## Answer

**7 signers** of the Declaration of Independence died before American independence was officially won (Treaty of Paris, September 3, 1783), excluding future presidents and anyone born outside the US.

### The 7 signers:

| Signer | State | Death Date | Birthplace (Colony) |
|--------|-------|------------|---------------------|
| **John Morton** | PA | April 1, 1777 | Ridley Township, Pennsylvania |
| **Philip Livingston** | NY | June 12, 1778 | Albany, New York |
| **John Hart** | NJ | May 11, 1779 | Stonington, Connecticut |
| **George Ross** | PA | July 14, 1779 | New Castle, Delaware |
| **Joseph Hewes** | NC | November 10, 1779 | Kingston, New Jersey |
| **Thomas Lynch Jr.** | SC | December 17, 1779 | Prince George Parish, South Carolina |
| **Richard Stockton** | NJ | February 28, 1781 | Princeton, New Jersey |

### Excluded from the 9 who died pre-1783:
- **Button Gwinnett** (died May 19, 1777) — born in Gloucester, **England**
- **George Taylor** (died February 23, 1781) — born in **Ireland**
- **John Adams** & **Thomas Jefferson** — future presidents, but both died in 1826 (well after independence)

### Key facts:
- **Independence officially won**: September 3, 1783 (Treaty of Paris signing — Britain formally recognized US sovereignty) [1][2]
- **Future presidents among signers**: Only John Adams and Thomas Jefferson [3]
- **Foreign-born signers (8)**: Gwinnett, Morris, Lewis, Smith, Taylor, Thornton, Wilson, Witherspoon [4]
- **Pre-independence deaths (9)**: Morton, Gwinnett, Livingston, Hart, Ross, Hewes, Lynch Jr., Stockton, Taylor [5]
