**Answer:** James A. Garfield (20th U.S. President)

- **Children:** 7 total (5 sons, 2 daughters) — exceeds 6  
- **First-born daughter:** Eliza Arabella Garfield (born July 3, 1860)  
- **Nickname:** “Little Trot” (also “Trottie”)  
- **Dickens character & book:** Miss Betsey Trotwood from *David Copperfield*  

The National Park Service confirms: “She was usually called ‘Little Trot,’ a variation of the name of a character [Mrs. Trotwood] in *David Copperfield* by Charles Dickens.”
