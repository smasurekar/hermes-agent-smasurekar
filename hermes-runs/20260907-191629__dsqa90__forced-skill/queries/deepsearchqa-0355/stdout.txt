Based on the official Elections Canada results for the 44th Canadian General Election (September 20, 2021), **only 2 of British Columbia's 42 electoral districts** had the Conservative candidate receive over 50% of valid votes:

| Electoral District | Conservative Votes | Total Valid Votes | Conservative % |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cariboo—Prince George** | 25,771 | 50,713 | **50.8%** |
| **Prince George—Peace River—Northern Rockies** | 29,882 | 49,197 | **60.7%** |

**Sources:**
- Elections Canada, 44th General Election, Table 12 — Complete candidate results by riding: https://www.elections.ca/res/rep/off/ovr2021app/53/table12E.html [1]
- Wikipedia "2021 Canadian federal election in British Columbia" (Synopsis by riding table, citing Elections Canada tables 3, 8, 9): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Canadian_federal_election_in_British_Columbia [2]

**Note:** The next-highest Conservative vote shares in BC were Central Okanagan—Similkameen—Nicola (47.6%), Abbotsford (47.9%), and North Okanagan—Shuswap (46.4%) — all below the 50% threshold.
