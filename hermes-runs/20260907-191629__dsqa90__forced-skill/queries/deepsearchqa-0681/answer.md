Based on the official EIA Electric Power Annual 2020, Table 3.8 (Utility Scale Facility Net Generation from Coal by State, by Sector), here are the top 5 states by coal electricity generation in 2020:

**Top 5 States for Coal Electricity Generation in 2020 (Thousand MWh):**

1. **Texas** – 78,825 thousand MWh
2. **Missouri** – 51,756 thousand MWh
3. **West Virginia** – 50,216 thousand MWh
4. **Indiana** – 47,773 thousand MWh
5. **Ohio** – 45,009 thousand MWh

**Source:** U.S. Energy Information Administration, Form EIA-923, Power Plant Operations Report — [Electric Power Annual 2020, Table 3.8](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/annual/archive/2020/html/epa_03_08.html) (All Sectors, Year 2020 column).

*Note: Kentucky ranked 6th at 43,638 thousand MWh, just below Ohio.*
