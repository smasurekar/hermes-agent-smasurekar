Based on Table 2.2 of the ICILS 2023 International Report (page 56), the countries with the **most categories** marked as "Available but not compulsory to teach" (◇) for **both** CIL and CT at ISCED 1 (primary) level are:

**Netherlands** and **United States** — each with **4 out of 4 categories** for CIL and **4 out of 4 categories** for CT (the maximum possible, since there are 4 subject categories per domain at ISCED 1).

Other notable countries with high counts in both domains:
- **Oman**: 3 CIL, 4 CT
- **Spain**: 2 CIL, 4 CT
- **Serbia**: 2 CIL, 3 CT
- **Austria**: 2 CIL, 3 CT

The table uses symbols: ◆ = Compulsory to teach, ◇ = Available but not compulsory to teach, − = Not taught. North Rhine-Westphalia (Germany) is a benchmarking participant, not a country.
