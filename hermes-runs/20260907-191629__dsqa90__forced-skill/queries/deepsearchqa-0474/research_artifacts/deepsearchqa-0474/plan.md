# Research Plan: 2007 Rock and Roll Hall of Fame Inductee with Longest-Charting Billboard Hot 100 Song (pre-Jan 2024)

## Goal
Identify which 2007 Rock and Roll Hall of Fame inductee had the longest-charting song on the Billboard Hot 100 prior to January 2024, and name both the artist and song.

## Components
1. **List all 2007 inductees** (performers category, possibly early influencers/sidemen)
2. **For each inductee, find their Billboard Hot 100 chart history** - specifically the song with the most weeks on the chart
3. **Compare chart durations** across all inductees' longest-charting songs
4. **Verify the winner** with authoritative Billboard data (pre-Jan 2024 cutoff)

## Constraints
- Only consider chart performance prior to January 2024
- Use Billboard Hot 100 (not other charts like Hot 100 Airplay, etc.)
- Focus on "weeks on chart" as the metric for "longest-charting"
- Cite authoritative sources (Billboard.com, official chart archives, reputable music databases)
