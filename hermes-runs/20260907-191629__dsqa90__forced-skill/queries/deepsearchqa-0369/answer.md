## Analysis

Both countries experienced significant GDP declines between 2008 and 2018 due to prolonged conflicts:
- **Syria**: Civil war began in 2011, causing GDP to collapse from ~$60B (2010) to ~$21B (2018)
- **Yemen**: Civil war escalated in 2014-2015, causing GDP to fall from ~$27B (2008) to ~$21.6B (2018)

No other Asian countries or territories in the database.earth dataset meet both thresholds. Other candidates with 2008 GDP > $25B (Lebanon, Azerbaijan, Sri Lanka, Bahrain) all had 2018 GDP well above $22B.
