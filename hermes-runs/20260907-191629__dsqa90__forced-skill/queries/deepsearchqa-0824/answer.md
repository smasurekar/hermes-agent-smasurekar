## CLRD in Top 5 Leading Causes of Death (2022)

According to CDC NCHS "Stats of the States" data (cited by Becker's Oncology), CLRD (chronic lower respiratory diseases, ICD-10 J40-J47) appears in the top 5 leading causes of death for 44 of 51 jurisdictions. The only jurisdictions where CLRD is **not** in the top 5 are: California, District of Columbia, Hawaii, Minnesota, Texas, Utah, and Washington [2][3].

## Intersection: All 5 Top Heart Disease States Have CLRD in Top 5

| State | Heart Disease Rank | CLRD Rank in Leading Causes | Top 5 Causes (for verification) |
|-------|-------------------|----------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Oklahoma | 1 | 4 | Heart disease, Cancer, Accidents, **CLRD**, Stroke |
| Mississippi | 2 | 3 | Heart disease, Cancer, **CLRD**, Accidents, Stroke |
| Alabama | 3 | 4 | Heart disease, Cancer, Stroke, **CLRD**, Accidents |
| Arkansas | 4 | 3 | Heart disease, Cancer, **CLRD**, Stroke, Accidents |
| Louisiana | 5 | 5 | Heart disease, Cancer, Accidents, Stroke, **CLRD** |

**All five states** that ranked in the top 5 for heart disease death rates in 2022 also had chronic lower respiratory diseases (CLRD) in their top 5 leading causes of death in 2022.
