## Notes
- All 11 states produced ≥1,000 gallons in 2017 (West Virginia at 93,672 gallons is the lowest among them)
- The 2012 Census Table 37 reports maple syrup production for 30 states; the 2017 data (via 2022 Census Table 37) covers 30 states with some changes in which states report
- Data are from the USDA NASS Census of Agriculture, conducted every 5 years, Table 37 "Maple Syrup" in the State Data chapter
- "(D)" = withheld to avoid disclosing individual operations; those states are excluded from this comparison
