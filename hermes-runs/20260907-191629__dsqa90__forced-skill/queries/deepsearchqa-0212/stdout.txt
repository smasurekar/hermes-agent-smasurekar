Based on the research, here are the fish that form mixed schools with Albacore and live 20 years or less:

## Fish that school with Albacore (lifespan ≤ 20 years)

| Common Name | Scientific Name | Max Recorded Age | Schooling Source | Lifespan Source |
|-------------|-----------------|------------------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Skipjack tuna** | *Katsuwonus pelamis* | 12 years | FAO Species Catalogue Vol. 2 (Collette & Nauen 1983), FishBase, NOAA Fisheries, Animal Diversity Web | FishBase Ref. 168 |
| **Yellowfin tuna** | *Thunnus albacares* | 18 years | FAO Species Catalogue Vol. 2 (Collette & Nauen 1983), FishBase, NOAA Fisheries, Animal Diversity Web | FishBase Ref. 137697 |

## Excluded species (lifespan > 20 years)

- **Southern bluefin tuna** (*Thunnus maccoyii*) — documented mixed-school associate per FishBase/FAO, but maximum age 20–40 years (FishBase, Gunn et al. 2008)

## Notes

- The three consistently documented mixed-school associates across FAO, NOAA, FishBase, and Animal Diversity Web are **Skipjack, Yellowfin, and Southern bluefin tuna**.
- Other species (Bigeye, Blackfin, Longtail, Frigate, Bullet, Kawakawa, Mahi-mahi, Wahoo, Rainbow runner, Triggerfish) were not confirmed as Albacore mixed-school associates in the authoritative sources searched.
- Mixed schools typically involve **juvenile/immature Albacore** and often associate with **floating objects** (Sargassum, FADs).
