## Answer: **Argentina** (joined the Allies on March 27, 1945)

### Supporting Evidence

**Step 1: League of Nations founding members (January 10, 1920)**
The 24 founding members on the League's first day were: Belgium, Bolivia, Brazil, British Empire (UK, Canada, Australia, South Africa, New Zealand, India), France, Guatemala, Italy, Japan, Panama, Peru, Poland, Siam (Thailand), Czechoslovakia, Uruguay, Argentina, Chile, Paraguay, Persia (Iran), Spain.

**Step 2: Original 51 UN members (1945)**
Authoritative list from the UN Dag Hammarskjöld Library: Republic of China, France, USSR, UK, USA, Argentina, Brazil, Byelorussian SSR, Chile, Cuba, Czechoslovakia, Denmark, Dominican Republic, Egypt, El Salvador, Haiti, Iran, Lebanon, Luxembourg, New Zealand, Nicaragua, Paraguay, Philippines, Poland, Saudi Arabia, Syria, Turkey, Ukrainian SSR, Yugoslavia, Australia, Belgium, Bolivia, Canada, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Ethiopia, Greece, Guatemala, Honduras, India, Iraq, Liberia, Mexico, Netherlands, Norway, Panama, Peru, South Africa, Uruguay, Venezuela.

**Step 3: Intersection (20 nations)**
Argentina, Australia, Belgium, Bolivia, Brazil, Canada, Chile, Czechoslovakia, France, Guatemala, India, Iran, New Zealand, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Poland, South Africa, United Kingdom, Uruguay.

**Step 4: WWII Allies joining dates for these 20 nations**
| Nation | Date Joined Allies | Event |
|--------|-------------------|-------|
| Argentina | **March 27, 1945** | Declared war on Germany and Japan (Decree No. 6945/45) |
| Chile | February 12, 1945 | Adhered to Declaration by United Nations |
| Paraguay | February 12, 1945 | Adhered to Declaration by United Nations |
| Peru | February 11, 1945 | Adhered to Declaration by United Nations |
| Uruguay | February 23, 1945 | Adhered to Declaration by United Nations |
| Bolivia | April 27, 1943 | Adhered to Declaration by United Nations |
| Brazil | February 8, 1943 | Adhered to Declaration by United Nations |
| Iran | September 10, 1943 | Adhered to Declaration by United Nations |
| France | December 26, 1944 | Adhered to Declaration by United Nations |
| 11 others | January 1, 1942 | Original signatories of the Declaration by United Nations |

**Step 5: Latest joiner**
Argentina was the last of these 20 nations to formally join the Allies, declaring war on Germany and Japan on **March 27, 1945** — just weeks before Germany's surrender on May 8, 1945. Argentina never signed the Declaration by United Nations during the war; its formal adherence came solely through this late declaration of war.
