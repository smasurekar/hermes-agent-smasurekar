# Research Plan: CDC 2022 Death Rates by US State

## Goal
Identify US states that had ALL THREE of these death rates in 2022 (per CDC data):
- Heart disease > 191.5 per 100,000
- Cancer > 161.9 per 100,000
- Diabetes > 33.1 per 100,000

## Components
1. CDC 2022 age-adjusted death rates for heart disease by state
2. CDC 2022 age-adjusted death rates for cancer by state
3. CDC 2022 age-adjusted death rates for diabetes by state
3. Cross-reference all three to find states meeting all thresholds

## Constraints
- Use official CDC WONDER / CDC Data Hub / NCHS data
- Age-adjusted rates per 100,000 population
- Year: 2022 specifically
- Output: list of state names

## Queries
1. **High** - CDC 2022 age-adjusted heart disease death rates by state
2. **High** - CDC 2022 age-adjusted cancer death rates by state
3. **High** - CDC 2022 age-adjusted diabetes death rates by state
