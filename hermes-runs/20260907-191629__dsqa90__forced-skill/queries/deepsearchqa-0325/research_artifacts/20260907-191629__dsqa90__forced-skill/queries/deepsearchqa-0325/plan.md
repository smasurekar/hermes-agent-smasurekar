# Research Plan

## Goal
Identify which of the top 5 largest economies in 2020 (per Madison Trust) had coronary artery disease as the #1 cause of death for males in both 2020 and 2021 (per WHO).

## Components
1. **Top 5 economies 2020 (Madison Trust)** — Identify the 5 countries
2. **WHO cause-of-death data 2020** — For each of the 5 countries, get male cause-of-death rankings
3. **WHO cause-of-death data 2021** — For each of the 5 countries, get male cause-of-death rankings
4. **Cross-reference** — Determine which country(s) meet the criteria in both years

## Constraints
- Must use Madison Trust for GDP ranking (not IMF, World Bank, etc.)
- Must use WHO for cause-of-death data (not CDC, national stats, etc.)
- Answer must cite sources for each fact
- Only countries in the top 5 per Madison Trust are in scope
