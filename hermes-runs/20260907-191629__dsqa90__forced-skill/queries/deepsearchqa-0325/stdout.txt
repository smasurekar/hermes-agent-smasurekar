## WHO Cause-of-Death Data (Males)

### 2020 (WHO Global Health Estimates 2021)
| Country | #1 Cause of Death (Males) | IHD Rank |
|---------|---------------------------|----------|
| United States | **Ischaemic heart disease** (285.4k) | #1 ✓ |
| China | Stroke (1,436.9k) | #2 |
| Japan | **Ischaemic heart disease** (80.5k) | #1 ✓ |
| Germany | **Ischaemic heart disease** (89.4k) | #1 ✓ |
| United Kingdom | COVID-19 (45.3k) | #2 |

Source: WHO GHE 2020 data — `https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/gho-documents/global-health-estimates/ghe2021_deaths_bycountry_2020.xlsx`

### 2021 (WHO Global Health Estimates 2021)
| Country | #1 Cause of Death (Males) | IHD Rank |
|---------|---------------------------|----------|
| United States | **Ischaemic heart disease** (289.1k) | #1 ✓ |
| China | Stroke (1,437.2k) | #2 |
| Japan | **Ischaemic heart disease** (85.3k) | #1 ✓ |
| Germany | **Ischaemic heart disease** (91.2k) | #1 ✓ |
| India* | COVID-19 (2,032.3k) | #2 |

Source: WHO GHE 2021 data — `https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/gho-documents/global-health-estimates/ghe2021_deaths_bycountry_2021.xlsx`

*India was not in the top 5 per Madison Trust (ranked 6th).

---

## Answer

**Three countries** — **United States, Japan, and Germany** — had coronary artery disease (ischaemic heart disease) as the #1 cause of death for males in **both 2020 and 2021**, according to WHO data.
