## Calculation of Improvements (2012 → 2022)

Only **Reading** and **Writing** meet the ≥91% participation threshold in 2022:

- **Reading**: 542.6 − 541.3 = **+1.3 points**
- **Writing**: 529.8 − 517.9 = **+11.9 points**

## Answer

**Writing** had the greatest improvement in average NAPLAN score for Year 7 between 2012 and 2022 (+11.9 points), and its 2022 participation rate was 91.9% (≥ 91%).
