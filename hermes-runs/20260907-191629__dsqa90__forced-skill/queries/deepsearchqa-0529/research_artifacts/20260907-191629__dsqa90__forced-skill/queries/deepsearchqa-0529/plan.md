# Research Plan: Year 7 NAPLAN 2012 vs 2022 Comparison

## Goal
Find which Year 7 NAPLAN subject had the greatest improvement in average score between 2012 and 2022, with 2022 participation rate ≥ 91%.

## Components
1. **2012 Year 7 average scores by subject** - Reading, Writing, Spelling, Grammar & Punctuation, Numeracy
2. **2022 Year 7 average scores by subject** - Same subjects
3. **2022 Year 7 participation rates by subject** - Must be ≥ 91%
4. **Calculate improvements** - Difference between 2022 and 2012 averages per subject
5. **Identify winner** - Subject with max improvement meeting participation threshold

## Constraints
- Data must come from ACARA National Report on Schooling (naplan-national-report-archive)
- Australia-wide (not state/territory specific)
- Year 7 only
- Participation rate for 2022 must be ≥ 91%

## Queries
1. **High depth**: ACARA NAPLAN 2012 national report Year 7 average scores by subject (Reading, Writing, Spelling, Grammar & Punctuation, Numeracy)
2. **High depth**: ACARA NAPLAN 2022 national report Year 7 average scores by subject
3. **High depth**: ACARA NAPLAN 2022 national report Year 7 participation rates by subject
4. **Medium depth**: Verify NAPLAN subject list for Year 7 (confirm 5 subjects tested)
5. **Low depth**: Check if any methodology changes between 2012-2022 affect comparability
