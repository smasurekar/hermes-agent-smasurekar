Based on my research, **Endeavour (OV-105)** has the second fewest construction milestones (10) before 2024, with only Enterprise (9) having fewer.

## Endeavour Missions Landing at Edwards Air Force Base (EAFB)

From NASA's official "Flights of Endeavour" document, the following 7 missions landed at EAFB:

| Mission | Launch Date | Landing Date | Primary Payload |
|---------|-------------|--------------|-----------------|
| **STS-49** | May 7, 1992 | **May 16, 1992 at EAFB** | Rescue, repair, redeploy INTELSAT VI (F-3) |
| **STS-59** | April 9, 1994 | **April 20, 1994 at EAFB** | Space Radar Laboratory-1 (SRL-1) |
| **STS-68** | September 30, 1994 | **October 11, 1994 at EAFB** | Space Radar Laboratory-2 (SRL-2) |
| **STS-67** | March 2, 1995 | **March 18, 1995 at EAFB** | Astro-2 |
| **STS-100** | April 19, 2001 | **May 1, 2001 at EAFB** | ISS Mission — SSRMS (Canadarm2), MPLM Raffaello, UHF Antenna |
| **STS-111** | June 5, 2002 | **June 19, 2002 at EAFB** | ISS Mission — Mobile Remote Service Base System, MPLM Leonardo |
| **STS-126** | November 14, 2008 | **November 30, 2008 at EAFB** | ISS Mission — MPLM Leonardo |

**Total: 7 EAFB landings** out of 25 total Endeavour flights (STS-134 was scheduled but not yet flown at time of this document).
