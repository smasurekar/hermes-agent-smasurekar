# Research Plan: NASA Space Shuttle Construction Milestones & EAFB Landings

## Goal
Identify the Space Shuttle with the second fewest construction milestones before 2024, then list its missions that landed at Edwards Air Force Base (EAFB).

## Components
1. **NASA Space Shuttle Reference Guide** — Locate the authoritative NASA reference guide detailing all Space Shuttles with construction milestones
2. **Construction Milestones per Shuttle** — Extract construction milestone counts for each orbiter (Enterprise, Columbia, Challenger, Discovery, Atlantis, Endeavour) before 2024
3. **Rank Shuttles by Milestone Count** — Determine which shuttle has the second fewest milestones
4. **Flight History of Target Shuttle** — Get complete flight manifest for that shuttle
5. **EAFB Landing Missions** — Filter flights to those landing at Edwards Air Force Base

## Constraints
- Use only NASA or authoritative government sources
- Construction milestones must be dated before 2024
- Output: ranked list of shuttles by milestone count, then EAFB missions for the 2nd fewest
