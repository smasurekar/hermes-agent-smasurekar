**Answer: Lialis**

**Reasoning:**

1. **Gecko families and species diversity (from Zheng & Wiens 2016 and supplementary data):**
   - Carphodactylidae: 31 species (least diverse)
   - Pygopodidae: 45 species
   - Eublepharidae: 38 species
   - Sphaerodactylidae: 224 species
   - Phyllodactylidae: 148 species
   - Diplodactylidae: 154 species
   - Gekkonidae: 1,281 species (most diverse)

2. **Phylogenetic relationships:** According to Zheng & Wiens (2016) and corroborating studies (Pyron et al. 2013), **Carphodactylidae is the sister group to Pygopodidae**, and this clade is sister to Diplodactylidae (forming Pygopodoidea).

3. **Northernmost genus of Pygopodidae:** The family Pygopodidae is almost entirely restricted to Australia, except for the genus **Lialis** which has two species (Lialis burtonis and Lialis jicari) that extend into New Guinea and nearby islands (Aru Islands, Bismarck Archipelago). All other pygopodid genera (Aprasia, Delma, Ophidiocephalus, Paradelma, Pletholax, Pygopus) are endemic to Australia.
