**EPCOT** saw the largest growth in attendance between 2022 and 2023.

### 2022 vs. 2023 Attendance by Park (TEA/AECOM data)

| Park | 2022 Attendance | 2023 Attendance | Absolute Change | % Change |
|------|----------------|----------------|-----------------|----------|
| **EPCOT** | 10,000,000 | 11,980,000 | **+1,980,000** | **+19.8%** |
| Magic Kingdom | 17,133,000 | 17,720,000 | +587,000 | +3.4% |
| Disney's Hollywood Studios | 10,900,000 | 10,300,000 | –600,000 | –5.5% |
| Disney's Animal Kingdom | 9,027,000 | 8,770,000 | –257,000 | –2.8% |

EPCOT was the only park to show significant growth—nearly 2 million additional visitors, a 19.8% increase year-over-year. The other three parks all saw declines.
