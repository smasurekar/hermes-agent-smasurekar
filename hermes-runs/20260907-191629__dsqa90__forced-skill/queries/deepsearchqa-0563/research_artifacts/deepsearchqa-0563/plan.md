# Research Plan: Austin Artists in 2023 The Art of Texas State Parks Exhibit

## Goal
Identify which artists who participated in the 2023 "The Art of Texas State Parks" traveling art exhibit were from Austin, Texas.

## Components
1. **Exhibit verification** - Confirm details about the 2023 "The Art of Texas State Parks" traveling exhibit
2. **Artist roster** - Find the complete list of participating artists
3. **Artist locations** - Determine which artists are based in/hail from Austin, Texas
4. **Cross-reference** - Match Austin-based artists to the exhibit roster

## Constraints
- Output: List of Austin artists with source citations
- Focus: 2023 exhibit only (not other years)
- Geography: Austin, Texas specifically (not just Texas generally)

## Queries

### Query 1 (High depth): Exhibit overview and artist roster
**Search for:** "The Art of Texas State Parks 2023 traveling exhibit artists list"
**Target:** Official Texas Parks & Wildlife site, TPWD press releases, exhibit catalogs, museum pages hosting the exhibit

### Query 2 (High depth): Individual artist profiles and locations
**Search for:** "[Artist name] Austin Texas artist" for each artist found in Query 1
**Target:** Artist websites, gallery representations, bios, interviews mentioning Austin base

### Query 3 (Medium depth): Exhibit catalog or press kit
**Search for:** "The Art of Texas State Parks 2023 catalog PDF" OR "Art of Texas State Parks artist roster 2023"
**Target:** PDF catalogs, press kits, exhibition checklists from host venues

### Query 4 (Medium depth): News coverage of exhibit
**Search for:** "Art of Texas State Parks 2023 exhibit Austin artists" OR "Texas State Parks art exhibit 2023 participating artists"
**Target:** Texas Monthly, Austin American-Statesman, local arts coverage, museum blogs

### Query 5 (Low depth): Texas Parks & Wildlife official page
**Search for:** site:tpwd.texas.gov "Art of Texas State Parks" 2023
**Target:** Official TPWD exhibit page with artist information
