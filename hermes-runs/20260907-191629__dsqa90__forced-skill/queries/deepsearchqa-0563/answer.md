Based on the deep research across multiple official sources (TPWD press releases, Bullock Museum pages, Texas A&M Press catalog, and news coverage), **four artists** from the 2023 "The Art of Texas State Parks" traveling exhibit were from Austin, Texas:

## Austin-Based Artists in the 2023 Exhibit

| Artist | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Fidencio Duran** | Austin-based painter & muralist; painted LBJ State Park (Sauer-Beckmann Living History Farm) |
| **Malou Flato** | Longtime Austin resident with studio in Austin + Hill Country ranch; painted Pedernales Falls State Park |
| **Gordon Fowler** | Native Texan, Austin-based 50+ years; taught watercolor at UT & The Contemporary Austin; painted Bastrop State Park and Honey Creek State Natural Area |
| **Terri M. Wells** | Listed as Austin-based in TPWD/CBS Austin rosters |
