- **Subject:** New Mexico landscape with Penitente cross, Taos area
- **Acquisition:** Art Institute Purchase Fund, 1943
- **Status:** On View, Gallery 161
- **Verification:**

### **2. Red Hills with Flowers (1937)**

- **Medium:** Oil on canvas (50.8 × 63.5 cm / 20 × 25 in.)
- **Subject:** New Mexico landscape — red hills near Ghost Ranch/Abiquiú with blanket flowers
- **Acquisition:** Bequest of Hortense Henry Prosser, 1992 (originally exchanged to Prosser in 1963)
- **Status:** Currently Off View
- **Verification:**

### **3. Red and Pink Rocks and Teeth (1938)**
- **AIC Accession:** 1955.1223
- **Medium:** Oil on canvas (53.3 × 33 cm / 21 × 13 in.)
- **Subject:** New Mexico desert landscape elements — stacked red/pink rocks and bleached jawbone with teeth, found at Ghost Ranch
- **Acquisition:** Alfred Stieglitz Collection, gift of Georgia O'Keeffe, 1955
