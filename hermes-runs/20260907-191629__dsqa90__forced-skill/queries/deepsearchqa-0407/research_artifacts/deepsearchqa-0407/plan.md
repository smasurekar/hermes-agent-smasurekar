# Research Plan: Top 5 States by Car Crash Deaths (2023) with Seat Belt Use < 90%

## Goal
Identify which of the top 5 states with the most motor vehicle crash deaths in 2023 had a seat belt use rate below 90% in 2023.

## Components
1. **Top 5 states by motor vehicle crash deaths in 2023** - Need authoritative source (NHTSA FARS, IIHS, or CDC WONDER)
2. **Seat belt use rates by state for 2023** - Need NHTSA seat belt use survey data
3. **Cross-reference** - Match the two datasets and filter for states with < 90% seat belt use

## Constraints
- Data must be from 2023 (not 2022 or 2024)
- Sources must be authoritative government/transportation safety organizations
- Output: List of qualifying states with both metrics cited

## Queries
1. **High depth**: "2023 motor vehicle crash deaths by state NHTSA FARS" - Get top 5 states by total fatalities
2. **High depth**: "2023 seat belt use rate by state NHTSA" - Get seat belt use percentages for all states
3. **Medium depth**: "IIHS 2023 fatality facts by state" - Cross-check fatality numbers
4. **Medium depth**: "NHTSA 2023 seat belt use survey results" - Verify seat belt data source
5. **Low depth**: "CDC WONDER 2023 motor vehicle deaths by state" - Alternative fatality source
