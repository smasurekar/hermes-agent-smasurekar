# Research Plan: States with High Alcohol-Impaired Driving Fatalities and Excessive Drinking Rates (2022)

## Goal
Identify U.S. states that had BOTH:
1. Alcohol-impaired-driving traffic fatalities > 35% in 2022 (NHTSA Traffic Safety Facts)
2. Excessive drinking rate > 20% in 2022 (WorldPopulationReview.com)

## Components
1. **NHTSA Data**: State-by-state percentage of alcohol-impaired-driving traffic fatalities for 2022
2. **WorldPopulationReview Data**: State-by-state excessive drinking rates for 2022
3. **Cross-reference**: Identify states meeting both thresholds

## Constraints
- Use only 2022 data from both sources
- NHTSA source: Traffic Safety Facts annual report
- WorldPopulationReview source: Their state rankings page
- Output: List of qualifying states with both percentages cited

## Queries

### Query 1 (High Depth) - NHTSA 2022 Alcohol-Impaired Driving Fatalities by State
Find the NHTSA Traffic Safety Facts 2022 report table showing percentage of alcohol-impaired-driving fatalities by state. Need the specific percentage for each state.

### Query 2 (High Depth) - WorldPopulationReview 2022 Excessive Drinking Rates by State
Find WorldPopulationReview.com page showing excessive drinking rates by state for 2022. Need the percentage for each state.

### Query 3 (Medium Depth) - Verify NHTSA Data Source
Confirm the exact NHTSA report name and table number for 2022 alcohol-impaired driving fatalities by state.

### Query 4 (Medium Depth) - Verify WorldPopulationReview Data Source
Confirm the exact WorldPopulationReview page/URL for 2022 excessive drinking rates by state.

### Query 5 (Low Depth) - Cross-reference Method
Determine how to match state names/abbreviations between the two sources for accurate comparison.
