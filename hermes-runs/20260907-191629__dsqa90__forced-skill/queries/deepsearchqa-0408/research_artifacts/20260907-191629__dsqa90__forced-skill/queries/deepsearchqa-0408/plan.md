# Research Plan: Highest CO2 per capita among "very high" GPI countries (filtered by area)

## Goal
Identify which country with a "very high" 2023 Global Peace Index rating (and area between 25,000–1,000,000 km²) had the highest CO2 emissions per capita in 2023 per World Bank data.

## Components
1. **List of "very high" GPI 2023 countries** — Get the full list of countries rated "very high" in the 2023 Global Peace Index.
2. **Country areas** — Find land area (km²) for each country to filter out >1,000,000 km² or <25,000 km².
3. **2023 CO2 emissions per capita (World Bank)** — Retrieve CO2 emissions per capita (metric tons) for 2023 from data.worldbank.org for the filtered countries.
4. **Comparison and answer** — Rank the remaining countries by CO2 per capita and identify the highest.

## Constraints
- Use authoritative sources: Global Peace Index 2023 report, World Bank data.worldbank.org.
- Area filter: 25,000 km² ≤ area ≤ 1,000,000 km².
- Year: 2023 for both GPI and CO2 data.
- Output: Single country name with supporting citations.

## Queries
1. **High depth**: "2023 Global Peace Index very high rating countries list" — Get the official list of countries rated "very high" in GPI 2023.
2. **Medium depth**: "Country land area km2 list 2023" — Find land area for each candidate country to apply the filter.
3. **High depth**: "World Bank CO2 emissions per capita 2023 data.worldbank.org" — Retrieve 2023 CO2 per capita for each filtered country.
4. **Low depth**: "Global Peace Index 2023 methodology very high threshold" — Verify the "very high" rating definition/cutoff.
