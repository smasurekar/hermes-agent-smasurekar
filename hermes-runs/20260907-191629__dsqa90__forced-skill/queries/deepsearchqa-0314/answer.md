Based on the research from five parallel sub-agents covering authoritative databases, primary literature, and taxonomic reviews, here is the exhaustive list of all *Tyrannosaurus* species names proposed as of May 2024, with their taxonomic status.

---

## Exhaustive List of *Tyrannosaurus* Species (Status as of May 2024)

| Species | Authority | Status (May 2024) | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| ***Tyrannosaurus rex*** | Osborn, 1905 | **Valid — Type species** | Universally accepted; only species recognized by consensus for >100 years. All historical synonyms (*Dynamosaurus imperiosus*, *Manospondylus gigas*, *Albertosaurus megagracilis*, etc.) synonymized under *T. rex*. [1][2] |
| ***Tyrannosaurus mcraeensis*** | Dalman et al., 2024 | **Provisionally valid / Contested** | Described Jan 2024 (*Sci. Rep.* 13:22124) from Hall Lake Formation, NM. Listed as valid in PBDB [3]. **Contested**: Voris et al. (2025) and Carr argue differences fall within *T. rex* variation. [1][4] |
| ***Tyrannosaurus imperator*** | Paul et al., 2022 | **Invalid — Junior synonym of *T. rex*** | Proposed in 3-species hypothesis (*Evol. Biol.* 2022). **Formally rebutted** by Carr et al. (2022) (*Evol. Biol.*): variation unexceptional, statistical flaws, only 5/53 specimens assignable. Not accepted by tyrannosaur specialists (Brusatte, Holtz, Hone, Zanno, O'Connor). [5][6] |
| ***Tyrannosaurus regina*** | Paul et al., 2022 | **Invalid — Junior synonym of *T. rex*** | Same as *T. imperator* — rejected by same rebuttal and expert consensus. Paul maintains via postorbital boss morphology (2025), but remains minority view. [5][6] |
| ***Tyrannosaurus turpanensis*** | Zhai et al., 1978 | **Invalid — *Nomen nudum* / *Tarbosaurus* synonym** | Fragmentary Xinjiang material. Holtz (2004) treats as *nomen nudum* and junior synonym of *Tarbosaurus bataar*. PBDB retains entry but consensus does not recognize it as *Tyrannosaurus*. [1][7] |
| ***Tyrannosaurus megagracilis*** | Paul, 1988 | **Invalid — *Nomen nudum* / Junior synonym of *T. rex*** | Originally *Albertosaurus megagracilis* (invalidly published). Recombined as *Dinotyrannus megagracilis* (Olshevsky et al. 1995). Represents juvenile *T. rex*. [8] |
| ***Tyrannosaurus bataar*** | Maleev, 1955 | **Reassigned — *Tarbosaurus bataar*** | Moved to *Tarbosaurus* (Rozhdestvensky 1965). Most workers maintain *Tarbosaurus* as separate genus. [8] |
| ***Nanotyrannus lancensis*** | Bakker et al., 1988 | **Unresolved — Active debate** | **Two camps as of May 2024**: (1) Juvenile *T. rex* (Carr 1999/2020, Woodward et al. 2020, Brusatte, Holtz); (2) Valid distinct genus (Longrich & Saitta 2024 *Fossil Studies*; Zanno & Napoli 2025 *Nature* — post-cutoff). **No consensus**; both names in active use. PBDB has not synonymized. [9][10] |

### Other Historical Names (All Invalid)

| Name | Status |
|------|--------|
| *T. lanpingensis* | *Nomen nudum* / *Nomen dubium* (Holtz 2004) |
| *T. luanchuanensis* | Junior synonym of *Tarbosaurus bataar* |
| *T. zhuchengensis* | *Nomen dubium* (holotype non-diagnostic) |
| *T. vannus*, *T. gigantus*, *T. stanwinstonorum* | *Nomina nuda* |
| *Manospondylus gigas* (Cope 1892) | Senior synonym, suppressed by ICZN (unused since 1899) |
| *Dynamosaurus imperiosus* (Osborn 1905) | Junior synonym of *T. rex* |
| *Stygivenator* spp. | Generally considered juvenile *T. rex* |

## Summary for May 2024

| Category | Count |
|----------|-------|
| **Universally valid** | 1 (*T. rex*) |
| **Provisionally valid (new, contested)** | 1 (*T. mcraeensis*) |
| **Formally rejected / invalid** | 4 (*T. imperator*, *T. regina*, *T. turpanensis*, *T. megagracilis*) |
| **Reassigned to other genera** | 1 (*T. bataar* → *Tarbosaurus*) |
