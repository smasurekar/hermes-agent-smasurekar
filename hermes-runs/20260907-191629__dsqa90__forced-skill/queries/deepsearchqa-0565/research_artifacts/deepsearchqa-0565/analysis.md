# Analysis: Rent-to-Income Ratio Changes (2016-2021)

## Raw Data from ABS Census QuickStats

| State | 2016 Median Rent | 2016 Median Income | 2021 Median Rent | 2021 Median Income |
|-------|------------------|-------------------|------------------|-------------------|
| NSW   | $380             | $1,486            | $420             | $1,829            |
| VIC   | $325             | $1,419            | $370             | $1,759            |
| QLD   | $330             | $1,402            | $365             | $1,675            |

## Calculated Ratios (Median Weekly Rent ÷ Median Weekly Household Income)

| State | 2016 Ratio | 2021 Ratio | Change (pp) |
|-------|------------|------------|-------------|
| NSW   | 380/1486 = **25.57%** | 420/1829 = **22.96%** | **-2.61 pp** |
| VIC   | 325/1419 = **22.90%** | 370/1759 = **21.03%** | **-1.87 pp** |
| QLD   | 330/1402 = **23.54%** | 365/1675 = **21.79%** | **-1.75 pp** |

## Answer

**Queensland** had the smallest decrease in the proportion of median weekly rent to median weekly household income from 2016 to 2021, with a decline of only **1.75 percentage points** (from 23.54% to 21.79%).

NSW saw the largest decrease (-2.61 pp), followed by Victoria (-1.87 pp).
