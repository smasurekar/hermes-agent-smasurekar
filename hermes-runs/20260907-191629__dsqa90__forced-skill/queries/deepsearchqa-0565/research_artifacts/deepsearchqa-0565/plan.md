# Research Plan: Australian Census Rent-to-Income Ratio Changes (2016-2021)

## Goal
Determine which of NSW, Victoria, or Queensland had the smallest decrease in the proportion of median weekly rent to median weekly household income from 2016 to 2021.

## Components
1. **Median weekly rent** for all private dwellings in NSW, VIC, QLD for 2016 and 2021
2. **Median weekly household income** for all persons in NSW, VIC, QLD for 2016 and 2021
3. **Calculate rent-to-income ratio** for each state in each year
4. **Calculate change** in ratio from 2016 to 2021 for each state
5. **Compare** the three states to identify smallest decrease

## Constraints
- Use official Australian Census data (ABS)
- "All persons" and "all private dwellings" scope
- Compare NSW, Victoria, Queensland only
- Time period: 2016 vs 2021 Census
- Output: single state name with supporting calculations

## Queries
1. **High depth**: Median weekly rent for all private dwellings by state (NSW, VIC, QLD) - 2016 Census
2. **High depth**: Median weekly rent for all private dwellings by state (NSW, VIC, QLD) - 2021 Census
3. **High depth**: Median weekly household income by state (NSW, VIC, QLD) - 2016 Census
4. **High depth**: Median weekly household income by state (NSW, VIC, QLD) - 2021 Census
5. **Medium depth**: ABS Census data tables or QuickStats confirming the above figures
