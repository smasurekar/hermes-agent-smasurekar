# Research Plan: FBI Most Wanted 2000-2001 Additions Convicted of Murder Within 10 Years

## Goal
Identify all individuals added to the FBI's Ten Most Wanted Fugitives list in 2000 or 2001 who were subsequently convicted of murder within 10 years of their placement on the list.

## Components
1. **List Identification** - Complete list of individuals added to FBI Ten Most Wanted in 2000 and 2001
2. **Case Outcomes** - For each person, determine capture/resolution details and conviction records
3. **Murder Conviction Verification** - Confirm murder convictions within 10-year window post-placement
4. **Timeline Verification** - Ensure conviction dates fall within 10 years of list addition

## Constraints
- Only include those added in calendar years 2000 or 2001
- Only include murder convictions (not just charges or other crimes)
- Conviction must occur within 10 years of list placement date
- Must be verified from authoritative sources (FBI, court records, reputable news)
