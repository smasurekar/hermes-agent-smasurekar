**Route 1 Spring Garden** is the Halifax Transit bus route that runs along Oxford Street and terminates at **Mumford Terminal** — the transit terminal located right next to the Walmart and Sobeys at the Halifax Shopping Centre on Mumford Road.

## Verification from official sources

**Route 1 Spring Garden runs the full length of Oxford Street** in both directions per Halifax Transit's official route descriptions:
- **Bridge Terminal → Mumford Terminal**: "...continue on Coburg Road, **right on Oxford Street**, left on Almon Street... continue to *Mumford Terminal Bay 2*"
- **Mumford Terminal → Bridge Terminal**: "Depart *Mumford Terminal Bay 2*... **right on Oxford Street**, left on Coburg Road..." [1]

**Mumford Terminal location**: Situated at 7010 Mumford Road in the Halifax Shopping Centre complex. Both Walmart (6990 Mumford Rd) and Sobeys (6990 Mumford Rd, West End Mall) are directly adjacent to the terminal — all three share the same mall complex [2, 3].

**Route 1 is currently active** as a "Corridor Route" offering frequent daily service (including evenings/weekends) in the official August 2026 route list [4].
