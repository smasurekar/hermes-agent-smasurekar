## Research Summary

### Step 1: 2021 Toyota Aqua Battery Chemistry
The 2021 Toyota Aqua (second generation, XP210) uses **bipolar nickel-metal hydride (NiMH) battery** for most trim grades (X, G, Z, GR Sport). This was confirmed by Toyota's official July 2021 press release stating "world's first vehicle to use a high-output bipolar nickel-hydrogen battery" and Toyota Industries' confirmation of "bipolar nickel-metal hydride batteries for hybrid vehicles... production for the Toyota Aqua beginning May 2021" [1][2].

### Step 2: 2020 Toyota SUVs with Same NiMH Chemistry
The 2020 Toyota SUVs using NiMH (matching the Aqua's chemistry):
- **2020 Toyota Highlander Hybrid** — NiMH confirmed by Green Car Reports
- **2020 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid** — Base/most trims use NiMH
- **2020 Toyota C-HR Hybrid** — NiMH
- **2020 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid** — NiMH

The 2020 Toyota Venza uses **Lithium-Ion** and does NOT share the chemistry [3].

### Step 4: Oil Filter Cap Assembly Part Number
For the **2020 Toyota Highlander Hybrid (2.5L A25A-FXS hybrid engine)**, the Toyota OEM oil filter cap assembly part number is **15620-36020** (supersedes 15620-36010 and 15620-0V010) [5][6][7].
