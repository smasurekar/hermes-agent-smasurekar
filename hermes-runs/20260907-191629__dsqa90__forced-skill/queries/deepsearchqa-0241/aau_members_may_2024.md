# AAU Member Universities as of May 2024

**Source:** Official AAU website (https://www.aau.edu/about/member-universities) and AAU press release "Six Leading Research Universities Join the Association of American Universities" (May 31, 2023)

**Note:** The six new members (Arizona State University, George Washington University, University of California Riverside, University of Miami, University of Notre Dame, University of South Florida) joined on June 1, 2023. No membership changes occurred between June 2023 and May 2024. The AAU membership as of May 2024 consists of **71 institutions** (69 U.S. and 2 Canadian universities).
