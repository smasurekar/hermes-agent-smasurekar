## Answer

**ArcBest Corporation** had the greatest reduction in operating expenses for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023.

- **Absolute reduction**: ArcBest reduced operating expenses by **$379.7 million**, compared to Matson's $237.6 million reduction. Saia actually *increased* operating expenses by $99.4 million.
- **Percentage reduction**: ArcBest achieved an **8.19%** reduction, compared to Matson's 7.95%.
