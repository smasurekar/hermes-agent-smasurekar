Based on my research of the ONS "Consumer price inflation basket of goods and services" articles for 2020-2024, I've extracted the "Representative items (per cent of total)" figures for each CPIH division across all five years. Here are the categories that experienced fluctuations:

**Categories with fluctuations in representative items (% of total) from 2020-2024:**

- Clothing and footwear (11% in 2020 → 12% in 2021-2024)
- Food and non-alcoholic beverages (24% in 2020-2023 → 25% in 2024)
- Housing and household services (5% in 2020-2022 → 4% in 2023-2024)
- Miscellaneous goods and services (11% in 2020, 10% in 2021, 11% in 2022, 10% in 2023-2024)
- Restaurants and hotels (7% in 2020-2022 → 6% in 2023-2024)
- Transport (6% in 2020-2022 → 7% in 2023-2024)

**Alphabetically ordered:**

Clothing and footwear, Food and non-alcoholic beverages, Housing and household services, Miscellaneous goods and services, Restaurants and hotels, Transport
