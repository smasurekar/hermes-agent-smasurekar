# Research Plan

## Goal
Identify which of the 56 signers of the Declaration of Independence died before American independence was officially won (Treaty of Paris, September 3, 1783), excluding future presidents and anyone born outside the US.

## Components
1. Complete list of 56 signers of the Declaration of Independence
2. Death dates for each signer
3. Birth places for each signer (to exclude those born outside US)
4. Future presidents among signers (to exclude them)
5. Determine which signers died before September 3, 1783

## Constraints
- Only include signers born in what is now the US (the 13 colonies/states)
- Exclude John Adams and Thomas Jefferson (both future presidents)
- American independence officially won = Treaty of Paris signed September 3, 1783
- Provide citations for every fact
