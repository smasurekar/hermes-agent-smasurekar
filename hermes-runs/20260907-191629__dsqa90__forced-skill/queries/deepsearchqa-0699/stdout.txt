## Signers who died before American independence was officially won (Sep 3, 1783), excluding future presidents and those born outside the US

**7 signers** meet all criteria:

| Signer | State Represented | Birthplace (Colony) | Death Date |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|------------|
| **John Morton** | Pennsylvania | Pennsylvania | c. 1777 |
| **Philip Livingston** | New York | New York | June 12, 1778 |
| **John Hart** | New Jersey | New Jersey | May 11, 1779 |
| **Joseph Hewes** | North Carolina | New Jersey (Kingston) | October 10, 1779 |
| **George Ross** | Pennsylvania | Delaware (New Castle) | July 14, 1779 |
| **Thomas Lynch Jr.** | South Carolina | South Carolina | c. 1779 |
| **Richard Stockton** | New Jersey | New Jersey | February 28, 1781 |

---

### Exclusions applied

1. **Future presidents (2):** John Adams, Thomas Jefferson — both died in 1826
2. **Born outside the US (8):** Button Gwinnett (England), Robert Morris (England), Francis Lewis (Wales), James Smith (Northern Ireland), George Taylor (Ireland), Matthew Thornton (Ireland), James Wilson (Scotland), John Witherspoon (Scotland)
   - Of these, **Button Gwinnett** (d. May 1777) and **George Taylor** (d. Feb 1781) died before Sep 3, 1783, but are excluded due to foreign birth

---

### Key dates
- **Treaty of Paris signed:** September 3, 1783 — the official end of the war and recognition of US independence [1][2]
- **Declaration signed:** August 2, 1776 (most signatures)
