## Five States with Highest Poverty Rate (2022)

The Center for American Progress (CAP) does not publish a static ranked table of state poverty rates for 2022. Their interactive poverty data tools allow exploration, but no single CAP article or report explicitly lists "the top 5 states with their exact 2022 poverty rates." However, multiple secondary sources citing U.S. Census Bureau 2022 ACS data consistently identify these five states as having the highest official poverty rates:

1. **Mississippi** (~17.8–19.6%)
2. **Louisiana** (~16.9–18.9%)
3. **New Mexico** (~17.3–18.5%)
4. **West Virginia** (~15.3–16.8%)
5. **Arkansas** (~15.7–15.9%)

## Grandparents with Disability Caring for Grandchildren Under 18 (2022 ACS 5-Year)

Using U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 2022 5-Year estimates (Table B10052_003E: "Grandparents with any disability responsible for own grandchildren under 18 years"), the counts for these five states are:

| State | Grandparents with Disability Responsible for Grandchildren <18 |
|-------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Louisiana** | **15,157** (±2,321) |
| Mississippi | 11,873 (±2,115) |
| Arkansas | 9,537 (±1,620) |
| New Mexico | 6,197 (±1,323) |
| West Virginia | 5,697 (±1,236) |

## Answer

**Louisiana** had the highest number of grandparents on disability status who were taking care of grandchildren under 18 years old among the five states with the highest poverty rates in 2022, with **15,157** such grandparents (margin of error ±2,321).
