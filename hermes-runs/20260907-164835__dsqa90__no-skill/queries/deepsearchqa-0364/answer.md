Based on the **Autism by the Numbers 2023 Annual Report** (Autism Speaks/Drexel University) and **Census.gov 2023 ACS 1-year estimates** (Appendix Table 1), here are the 10 states with the oldest average age of first autism intervention, ranked by their 2023 poverty rates (highest to lowest):

| Rank by Poverty | State | Avg Age of First Intervention (years) | 2023 Poverty Rate (%) |
|-----------------|-------|--------------------------------------|------------------------|
| 1 | Mississippi | 5.5 | 18.0 |
| 2 | West Virginia | 7.2 | 16.7 |
| 3 | Oklahoma | 5.3 | 15.9 |
| 4 | Ohio | 5.6 | 13.3 |
| 5 | Missouri | 5.1 | 12.0 |
| 6 | Montana | 5.9 | 11.7 |
| 7 | Iowa | 5.0 | 11.3 |
| 8 | Wisconsin | 5.1 | 10.7 |
| 9 | North Dakota | 6.0 | 9.8 |
| 10 | Colorado | 5.0 | 9.3 |

**Notes**
- Intervention ages come from the 2016–2019 National Survey of Children’s Health (NSCH), as reported in the Autism by the Numbers 2023 Annual Report (state profiles, “Age of services”).
- Poverty rates are from the 2023 American Community Survey 1-year estimates, Appendix Table 1 (“Number and Percentage of People in Poverty… 2023”).
- Connecticut also had a 5.0-year average intervention age (tied for 9th/10th), with a 2023 poverty rate of 10.3%.
