**President:** James A. Garfield (he and Lucretia had seven children)

**Daughter (first-born):** Eliza Arabella Garfield

**Nickname:** "Little Trot"

**Source:** *David Copperfield* by Charles Dickens — the nickname was a variation of the character **Mrs. Betsey Trotwood** (often called "Aunt Betsey" or "Mrs. Trotwood").
