Based on the 2021 Canadian federal election results in British Columbia, only **two** electoral districts had the Conservative candidate receive over 50% of valid ballots:

1. **Cariboo—Prince George** — 50.8% (25,771 of 50,713 valid ballots)
2. **Prince George—Peace River—Northern Rockies** — 60.7% (29,882 of 49,197 valid ballots)

All other Conservative-won ridings in BC fell below the 50% threshold (ranging from 36.7% in Pitt Meadows—Maple Ridge to 47.9% in Abbotsford).
