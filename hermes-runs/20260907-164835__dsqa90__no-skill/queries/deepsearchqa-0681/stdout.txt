In 2020, the five states with the greatest **coal-fired electricity generating capacity** (net summer capacity) were:

1. **Wyoming**
2. **West Virginia** 
3. **Pennsylvania**
4. **Illinois**
5. **Kentucky**

Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) data from Form EIA-860 and Electric Power Annual 2020, Table 4.7.C. These same five states also led in coal production (mining) in 2020, producing about 380 million short tons combined.
