Based on the data from the official Robert F. Kennedy Human Rights Wikipedia page (which lists Ripple of Hope Award laureates by year) and the Nobel Prize records:

**Three people meet both criteria:**

1. **Desmond Tutu** — Ripple of Hope Award in **2008**, Nobel Peace Prize in **1984**
2. **Al Gore** — Ripple of Hope Award in **2011**, Nobel Peace Prize in **2007** (joint with the IPCC)
3. **Muhammad Yunus** — Ripple of Hope Award in **2013**, Nobel Peace Prize in **2006** (joint with Grameen Bank)

All three received their Nobel Prizes before 2010 and their Ripple of Hope Awards before 2017.
