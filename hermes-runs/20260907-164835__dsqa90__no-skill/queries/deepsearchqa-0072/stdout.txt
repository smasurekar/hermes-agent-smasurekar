Based on the CDC's National Vital Statistics Report "Deaths: Final Data for 2022" (Table 17), four US states had age-adjusted death rates exceeding all three thresholds in 2022:

| State | Heart Disease | Cancer | Diabetes |
|-------|--------------|--------|----------|
| **Arkansas** | 224.1 | 168.1 | 36.0 |
| **Mississippi** | 248.0 | 178.4 | 34.7 |
| **Oklahoma** | 257.1 | 171.6 | 33.8 |
| **West Virginia** | 209.5 | 176.3 | 41.1 |

**Thresholds:** Heart disease >191.5, Cancer >161.9, Diabetes >33.1 (all age-adjusted rates per 100,000 U.S. standard population).

Source: CDC/NCHS, National Vital Statistics Reports Volume 74, Number 4 (June 10, 2025), Table 17 — age-adjusted death rates for 2022 by state for Diseases of heart (I00–I09,I11,I13,I20–I51), Malignant neoplasms (C00–C97), and Diabetes mellitus (E10–E14).
