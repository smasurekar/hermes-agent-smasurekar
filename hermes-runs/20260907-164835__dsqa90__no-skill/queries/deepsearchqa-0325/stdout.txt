**Top 5 economies in 2020 (Madison Trust):**
1. United States ($20.9T)
2. China ($14.7T)
3. Japan ($5.1T)
4. Germany ($3.8T)
5. United Kingdom ($2.7T)

**Countries with coronary artery disease (ischaemic heart disease) as #1 cause of death for males in both 2020 and 2021 (WHO):**

**United States, Germany, and United Kingdom** — 3 of the 5 countries.

**Evidence:**

- **United States**: CDC and WHO data confirm heart disease (ischaemic heart disease) remained the leading cause of death for males in both 2020 and 2021.

- **Germany**: German Federal Statistical Office (Destatis) and WHO data show coronary heart disease (CHD/ischaemic heart disease) is the leading cause of death, including for males in 2020/2021.

- **United Kingdom**: ONS/WHO data show ischaemic heart disease was the leading cause of death for males in 2021 and 2022, with the same pattern in 2020 (COVID-19 was the leading cause *overall* but not for males specifically).

**Countries that did NOT meet the criteria:**

- **China**: Stroke is consistently the #1 cause of death; ischaemic heart disease ranks #2 for males (WHO GHE, GBD studies).
- **Japan**: Alzheimer's/dementia and stroke rank above ischaemic heart disease for males in 2021 (WHO GHE/GBD 2021 data).
