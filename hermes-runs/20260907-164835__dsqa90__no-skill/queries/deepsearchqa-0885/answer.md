Based on my research, there are **three** UK-born individuals who won the Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences while affiliated with a UK-based institution between the award's inception (1969) and the first female winner (Elinor Ostrom, 2009), and who have fewer than 20 "Articles" on IDEAS/RePEc published before their award year:

| Laureate | Award Year | UK Institution at Award | Articles Before Award Year |
|----------|------------|-------------------------|----------------------------|
| **James Meade** | 1977 | University of Cambridge | **19** |
| **Richard Stone** | 1984 | University of Cambridge | **17** |
| **James Mirrlees** | 1996 | University of Oxford / University of Cambridge | **5** |

The fourth UK-born UK-affiliated laureate in this period, **John Hicks** (1972, Oxford), has **30** articles before 1972, exceeding the threshold.

Ronald Coase (1991) was affiliated with the University of Chicago (US) at the time of his award, and Clive Granger (2003) was at UC San Diego (US), so neither meets the UK-affiliation criterion.
