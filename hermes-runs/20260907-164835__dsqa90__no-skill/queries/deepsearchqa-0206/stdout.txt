**Lialis**

**Reasoning:**

1. **Gecko families in the paper**: The Zheng & Wiens (2016) paper recognizes 7 gecko families within Gekkota: Carphodactylidae, Diplodactylidae, Eublepharidae, Gekkonidae, Phyllodactylidae, Pygopodidae, and Sphaerodactylidae.

2. **Family with least species diversity**: According to species counts from the literature (e.g., Uetz et al. 2019, Encyclopedia of Arkansas), **Carphodactylidae** has the fewest species (~30-31 species, 7 genera).

3. **Sister family to Carphodactylidae**: The paper states that within the Pygopodoidea clade (Carphodactylidae + Diplodactylidae + Pygopodidae), the topology follows Pyron et al. (2013) in placing **Carphodactylidae as sister to Pygopodidae** (though with weak support).

4. **Northernmost genus of Pygopodidae**: Pygopodidae genera are primarily Australian endemics (Aprasia, Delma, Ophidiocephalus, Paradelma, Pletholax, Pygopus). The genus **Lialis** is the only one that extends into New Guinea (Indonesia's Western New Guinea and Papua New Guinea's Bismarck Archipelago), making it the northernmost genus in the family.

Thus, the northernmost genus of the sister family (Pygopodidae) to the least diverse gecko family (Carphodactylidae) is **Lialis**.
