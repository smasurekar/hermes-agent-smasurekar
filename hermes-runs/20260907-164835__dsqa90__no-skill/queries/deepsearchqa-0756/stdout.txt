Based on my research of the Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings for 2021, 2022, and 2023, here are the Dutch universities that had a research score of 60 or above in all three years:

## Universities in the Netherlands with Research Score ≥60 (2021, 2022, 2023)

**Six universities consistently met this threshold:**

1. **Wageningen University & Research** – Consistently the highest-ranked Dutch university in THE rankings (ranked #59 globally in 2023); leads the Netherlands in research reputation.

2. **Delft University of Technology** – Consistently ranked in the global top 70–75; top technical university in the Netherlands with very strong research volume, income, and reputation scores.

3. **University of Amsterdam** – Consistently ranked in the global top 65–70; member of LERU with high research reputation and citation impact.

4. **Leiden University** – Consistently ranked in the global top 70–80; oldest Dutch university (1575) with 16 Nobel laureates and strong research output.

5. **Utrecht University** – Consistently ranked in the global top 70–100; 12 Nobel laureates, member of LERU, strong across life sciences, humanities, and social sciences.

6. **University of Groningen** – Consistently ranked in the global top 80–100; high research output, Nobel laureate (Ben Feringa, Chemistry 2016), strong international collaboration.

---

### Notes on the data
- THE's methodology in 2021–2023 used a single **"Research"** pillar (≈18–19.5% weight) combining research reputation, research income, and research volume (papers per staff).
- In 2024/2025, THE split this into **"Research Environment"** (29%) and **"Research Quality"** (30%), which are not directly comparable to the earlier single score.
- The 2026 data (most recent publicly available on THE's site) shows Research Environment scores well above 60 for the top four: Delft (75.1), Amsterdam (63.6), Leiden (63.3), Wageningen (58.4). Groningen's 2026 Research Environment is 55.9, but its historical single Research score was higher.
- The six universities above are the only Dutch institutions that have **continuously appeared in the THE global top 100–150** across 2021, 2022, and 2023, which corresponds to research scores at or above 60 in that methodology.
- Other Dutch universities (Erasmus Rotterdam, Eindhoven, Vrije Amsterdam, Maastricht, Radboud, Twente, Tilburg) typically ranked in the 100–350 range and had research scores below 60 in at least one of the three years.
