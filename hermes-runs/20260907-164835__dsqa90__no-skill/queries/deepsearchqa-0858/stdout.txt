Based on the attendance data from MagicGuides and the TEA/AECOM Global Attractions Attendance Report, **EPCOT** saw the largest growth in attendance between 2022 and 2023.

**2022 → 2023 attendance by park:**
- **EPCOT**: 10,000,000 → 11,980,000 (**+1,980,000 / +19.8%**) — largest absolute and percentage gain
- **Magic Kingdom**: 17,133,000 → 17,720,000 (+587,000 / +3.4%)
- **Disney's Hollywood Studios**: 10,900,000 → 10,300,000 (−600,000 / −5.5%)
- **Disney's Animal Kingdom**: 9,027,000 → 8,770,000 (−257,000 / −2.8%)

EPCOT's growth was driven by its multi-year transformation, including new attractions like Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind (opened May 2022) and the ongoing EPCOT overhaul.
