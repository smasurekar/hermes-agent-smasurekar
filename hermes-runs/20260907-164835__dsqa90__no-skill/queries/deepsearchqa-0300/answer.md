Based on my research, I've traced the chain of references:

1. **The 2020 review** by Kucharik & Chang in *Clinical Reviews in Allergy & Immunology* (June 2020) references the **2008 publication** by Parapia & Jackson: "Ehlers-Danlos syndrome – a historical review" in *British Journal of Haematology*.

2. **The 2008 Parapia & Jackson review** references the **1998 classification** of Ehlers-Danlos syndromes: "Ehlers-Danlos syndromes: revised nosology, Villefranche, 1997" published in *American Journal of Medical Genetics* (1998), authored by **P. Beighton, A. De Paepe, B. Steinmann, P. Tsipouras, R. Wenstrup**.

3. **Peter Beighton** is the geneticist who published this 1998 classification.

4. According to **Beighton's own CV** (University of Cape Town), his **first publication in *The Practitioner*** was:

**"Amitriptyline poisoning treated by forced diuresis"** (with M. Hardingham) — *Practitioner* 197: 354-357; 1966.
