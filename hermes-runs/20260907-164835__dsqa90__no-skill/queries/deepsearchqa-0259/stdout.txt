Based on the Virginia DWR FY2020 license sales data and 2020 Census populations, the CSPDC member localities with populations under 30,000 that had **more than 1,000 National Forest Permits** issued are:

| Locality | Type | 2020 Census Population | National Forest Permits (FY2020) |
|----------|------|------------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Rockbridge County** | County | 22,650 | **1,121** |
| **Staunton** | City | 25,963 | **2,112** |
| **Waynesboro** | City | 22,359 | **1,577** |

The other CSPDC localities under 30,000 population fell short of 1,000 permits:
- Bath County: 962 permits (pop. 4,094)
- Highland County: 258 permits (pop. 2,300)
- Buena Vista: 785 permits (pop. 6,641)
- Lexington: 908 permits (pop. 7,456)

Augusta County (4,381 permits) and Rockingham County (5,461 permits) exceeded 1,000 but have populations over 30,000. Harrisonburg (2,171 permits) also exceeds 1,000 but its population is ~51,800.

**Source:** Virginia DWR FY2020 Fishing & Hunting License Sales data (National Forest Permit, code 472) cross-referenced with 2020 U.S. Census populations for CSPDC member localities.
