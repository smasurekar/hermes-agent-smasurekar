Based on the BRFSS Prevalence & Trends Data (2011-present) from CDC, here are the states with prevalence below 45% of adults who had at least one drink of alcohol within the past 30 days, between 2019 and 2022:

## States with prevalence below 45% in at least one year (2019-2022)

**Alabama (AL):** 2019: 42.2%, 2020: 44.2%, 2021: 40.3%, 2022: 43.6%

**Arkansas (AR):** 2019: 40.8%, 2020: 43.6%, 2021: 42.9%, 2022: 44.5%

**Guam (GU):** 2019: 41.6%, 2020: 41.4%, 2021: 39.8%, 2022: 44.1%

**Kentucky (KY):** 2019: 42.3%, 2021: 39.2%, 2022: 35.2% (2020 data: 45.1%)

**Mississippi (MS):** 2019: 39.4%, 2020: 39.1%, 2021: 40.0%, 2022: 42.3%

**Oklahoma (OK):** 2019: 42.0%, 2020: 40.3%, 2021: 41.4%, 2022: 43.5%

**Puerto Rico (PR):** 2019: 31.6%, 2020: 31.2%, 2021: 32.7%, 2022: 34.6%

**Tennessee (TN):** 2019: 44.8% (other years above 45%)

**Utah (UT):** 2019: 31.1%, 2020: 30.9%, 2021: 32.6%, 2022: 33.8%

**West Virginia (WV):** 2019: 37.4%, 2020: 36.6%, 2021: 37.0%, 2022: 39.7%

---
