**Sources & Notes:**

1. **Autism prevalence**: From Table 2 of Dietz, Rose, McArthur & Maenner (2020), "National and State Estimates of Adults with Autism Spectrum Disorder," *J Autism Dev Disord* 50(12):4258–4266. National average = 2.21%. All listed states meet or exceed this threshold.

2. **2023 unemployment**: From BLS "Annual average unemployment rates decreased in 6 states in 2023" (March 2024), Table "Unemployment rates by state, 2022–23 annual averages." All listed states have rates ≥ 3.2%.

3. **Electoral votes**: 
   - **2016**: Faithless Democratic electors occurred only in Washington (4 votes for Powell/Spotted Eagle), Hawaii (1 for Sanders), Colorado (1 attempted Kasich vote — replaced), Minnesota (1 attempted Sanders vote — replaced), and Maine (1 attempted Sanders vote — ruled out of order, voted Clinton on 2nd ballot). Colorado's elector was replaced, so its final certified slate was all-Clinton.
   - **2020**: No faithless electors nationwide. 
   - Maine and Nebraska are excluded because they split electoral votes by congressional district in both elections.
   - The 10 states above cast all electoral votes for Clinton (2016) and Biden (2020) in the final certified counts.

*Colorado's faithless elector was replaced before the final vote certification, so its electoral slate was unanimously Democratic in the official count.
