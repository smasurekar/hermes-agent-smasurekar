### Key Takeaways

- **Incineroar dominates offensively and in bulk** — +125 BST, crushing advantages in Attack (+70), HP (+35), and mixed offenses
- **Pachirisu wins only on Speed** (95 vs 60) and ties Sp. Def (90)
- **Role difference**: Pachirisu was a disruptive support (Follow Me redirect, Super Fang, Nuzzle) leveraging its Speed and Sp. Def; Incineroar is an offensive pivot with Intimidate, Fake Out, and massive Attack
- **BST gap**: Incineroar's 530 vs Pachirisu's 405 reflects the "pseudo-legendary-adjacent" starter power level vs a regional rodent
