From the NASA Space Shuttle reference guide, I counted the construction milestones for each shuttle:

- **Enterprise (OV-101)**: 9 milestones
- **Endeavour (OV-105)**: 11 milestones
- **Atlantis (OV-104)**: 12 milestones  
- **Challenger (OV-099)**: 19 milestones
- **Discovery (OV-103)**: 28 milestones
- **Columbia (OV-102)**: 30 milestones

The shuttle with the **second fewest** construction milestones is **Endeavour (OV-105)** with 11 milestones.

From the NASA "Flights of Endeavour" document, the missions where Endeavour landed at **EAFB (Edwards Air Force Base)** are:

1. **STS-49** (May 16, 1992) – Maiden flight; rescue, repair, redeploy INTELSAT VI
2. **STS-59** (April 20, 1994) – Space Radar Laboratory-1 (SRL-1)
3. **STS-68** (October 11, 1994) – Space Radar Laboratory-2 (SRL-2)
4. **STS-67** (March 18, 1995) – Astro-2
5. **STS-100** (May 1, 2001) – 9th ISS Mission; SSRMS; MPLM Raffaello; UHF Antenna
6. **STS-111** (June 19, 2002) – 14th ISS Mission; Mobile Remote Service Base System; MPLM Leonardo
7. **STS-126** (November 30, 2008) – 27th ISS Mission; MPLM Leonardo

**Total: 7 missions landed at EAFB.**
