Based on my research of the FBI's Ten Most Wanted Fugitives added in 2000 and 2001, here are the **five people** who were convicted of murder within ten years of their placement on the list:

---

### 1. **Jesse James Caston** (#459)
- **Added:** August 19, 2000
- **Convicted:** 2005 (pled guilty to second-degree murder, sentenced to life imprisonment at hard labor without benefit of probation, parole, or suspension of sentence) — *State v. Caston, 40,054-KA (La. Ct. App. 2005)*

---

### 2. **Aurlieas Dame McClarty** (#461)
- **Added:** February 5, 2001
- **Convicted:** May 2008 (convicted of two counts of first-degree murder and robbery with a firearm; sentenced to two consecutive life terms) — *Orlando Sentinel, May 14, 2008*

---

### 3. **Francis William Murphy** (#464)
- **Added:** June 6, 2001
- **Convicted:** November 2004 (convicted of five counts of second-degree murder and first-degree robbery after a non-jury trial; affirmed on appeal) — *People v. Murphy, 28 AD3d 1096 (N.Y. App. Div. 2006); Buffalo News, 2004*

---

### 4. **Dwight Bowen** (#465)
- **Added:** August 30, 2001
- **Convicted:** February 2004 (pled guilty to two counts of second-degree murder for the Molotov cocktail firebombing that killed two toddlers; sentenced to two consecutive life sentences) — *Commonwealth v. Bowen, CP-51-CR-0909801-2001; TribLive, Oct. 24, 2019*

---

### 5. **Felix Summers** (#468)
- **Added:** October 30, 2001
- **Convicted:** November 2006 (convicted by jury of first-degree murder and witness retaliation for the March 1999 killing of Charlotte Presley; sentenced to life imprisonment) — *Summers v. Wetzel, Civ. No. 13-3561 (E.D. Pa. 2016); Com. v. Summers, 2711 EDA 2014 (Pa. Super. Ct. 2016)*

---

### Excluded (not convicted of murder within 10 years):
- **Eric Franklin Rosser** (#460, Dec 2000) — convicted of child pornography offenses
- **Hopeton Eric Brown** (#462, Mar 2001) — killed in Jamaica in 2004 before conviction
- **Maghfoor Mansoor** (#463, May 2001) — killed in shootout with FBI in 2001
- **Nikolay Soltys** (#466, Aug 2001) — died by suicide in 2002
- **Clayton Lee Waagner** (#467, Sep 2001) — convicted of bank robbery, firearms violations, and anthrax threats, not murder
