**2021 proportions:**

**Decrease from 2016 to 2021 (percentage points):**
- NSW: 25.57% − 22.96% = **2.61 pp**
- Victoria: 22.90% − 21.03% = **1.87 pp**
- Queensland: 23.54% − 21.79% = **1.75 pp**
