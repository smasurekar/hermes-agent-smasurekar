Based on my research of the 2007 Rock and Roll Hall of Fame inductees' Billboard Hot 100 chart histories:

**R.E.M. and Van Halen are tied** for the longest-charting song among the 2007 inductees, with both songs spending **21 weeks** on the Billboard Hot 100:

- **R.E.M.** — "Losing My Religion" (1991): 21 weeks on chart, peaked at #4
- **Van Halen** — "Jump" (1984): 21 weeks on chart, peaked at #1

The other 2007 inductees' biggest Hot 100 hits charted for fewer weeks:
- **Patti Smith** — "Because the Night": 18 weeks (peaked #13)
- **The Ronettes** — "Be My Baby": 13 weeks (peaked #2)
- **Grandmaster Flash & the Furious Five** — "The Message": 7 weeks (peaked #62)
