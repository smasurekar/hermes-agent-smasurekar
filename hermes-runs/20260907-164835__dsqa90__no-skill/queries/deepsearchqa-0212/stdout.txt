Based on authoritative sources, albacore form mixed schools with **skipjack tuna, yellowfin tuna, bluefin tuna, and bigeye tuna**.

**Lifespans:**
- **Skipjack tuna**: 8–12 years (max 12)
- **Yellowfin tuna**: 6–8 years (max ~10)
- **Bigeye tuna**: 7–16 years (max ~16)
- **Bluefin tuna**: 20–50 years (exceeds 20)

**Excluding those that can live over 20 years (bluefin tuna), the remaining species are:**

1. **Skipjack tuna** (*Katsuwonus pelamis*)
2. **Yellowfin tuna** (*Thunnus albacares*)
3. **Bigeye tuna** (*Thunnus obesus*)
