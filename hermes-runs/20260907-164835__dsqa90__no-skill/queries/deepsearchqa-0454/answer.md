Based on the USDA NASS 2012 and 2017 Census of Agriculture data (Table 37 for maple syrup and the Specialty Crops reports), here are the maple-producing states that saw a decrease in gallons produced from 2012 to 2017 while still producing at least 1,000 gallons in both census years:

**Ohio** — 94,133 gallons (2012) → 93,672 gallons (2017)

**Virginia** — 1,800 gallons (2012) → 1,385 gallons (2017)

**Illinois** — 3,331 gallons (2012) → 2,384 gallons (2017)

All other maple-producing states (Connecticut, Maine, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, New Hampshire, New York, Pennsylvania, Vermont, Wisconsin, Indiana, West Virginia) showed production increases between the two census years.
