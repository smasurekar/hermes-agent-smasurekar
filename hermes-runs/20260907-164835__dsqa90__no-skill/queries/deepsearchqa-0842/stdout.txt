Based on the research, the "earlier case" Justice Gorsuch referenced in his *Denezpi* dissent is **Gamble v. United States** (2019). In *Gamble*, Justice **Ruth Bader Ginsburg** also dissented. In her dissent, she explicitly identified the dual-sovereignty precedent as having been **twice affirmed by the Court in 1959**—first in *Abbate v. United States* and then in *Bartkus v. Illinois*—and stated she would not adhere to “those ill-advised decisions.”

**The cases she felt should be overturned:**
- **Abbate v. United States**, 359 U.S. 187 (1959)  
- **Bartkus v. Illinois**, 359 U.S. 121 (1959)

Ginsburg’s dissent reads: “In *United States v. Lanza*, 260 U. S. 377 (1922), this Court held that… Decades later, a sharply divided Court reaffirmed this separate-sovereigns doctrine. *Abbate v. United States*, 359 U. S. 187 (1959); *Bartkus v. Illinois*, 359 U. S. 121 (1959). **I would not cling to those ill-advised decisions.**”
