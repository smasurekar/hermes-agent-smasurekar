Based on my research, the U.S. Presidents who meet all three criteria (attended a university that was an AAU member as of May 2024, had prior experience as a state governor, and were elected president after 1900) are:

**Confirmed (undergraduate at AAU member):**

1. **Theodore Roosevelt** — Harvard University (AAU founding member, 1900); Governor of New York (1899–1900); elected President in 1904.
2. **Woodrow Wilson** — Princeton University (AAU founding member, 1900); Governor of New Jersey (1911–1913); elected President in 1912.
3. **Franklin D. Roosevelt** — Harvard University (AAU founding member, 1900); Governor of New York (1929–1932); elected President in 1932.
4. **Jimmy Carter** — Georgia Institute of Technology (attended 1942–1943; Georgia Tech joined AAU in 2010); Governor of Georgia (1971–1975); elected President in 1976.
5. **George W. Bush** — Yale University (AAU founding member, 1900; BA 1968) and Harvard University (AAU founding member, 1900; MBA 1975); Governor of Texas (1995–2000); elected President in 2000.

**Conditional (graduate school at AAU member):**

6. **Bill Clinton** — Yale Law School (Yale University, AAU founding member, 1900; JD 1973); Governor of Arkansas (1979–1981, 1983–1992); elected President in 1992. *His undergraduate degree (Georgetown) and Rhodes Scholarship (Oxford) are not AAU institutions.*

**Did not meet the AAU criterion:**

- **Calvin Coolidge** — Amherst College (not an AAU member); Governor of Massachusetts; elected 1924.
- **Ronald Reagan** — Eureka College (not an AAU member); Governor of California; elected 1980.

**Summary:** Five presidents clearly qualify on undergraduate attendance; a sixth (Clinton) qualifies if graduate attendance at an AAU university counts.
