Earliest IUCN Red List assessment dates:

1. **1986** – Wild Water Buffalo (*Bubalus arnee*), Saiga tatarica, Komodo Dragon (*Varanus komodoensis*) — all first listed as Rare/Endangered
2. **1994** – Yellow-eared Parrot (*Ognorhynchus icterotis*)
3. **2012** – Siberian Taimen (*Hucho taimen*)

Sources: Wild Water Buffalo listed Endangered since 1986 (Wikipedia); Komodo Dragon previously assessed as Rare in 1986, 1988, 1990, 1994 (IUCN Red List history); Saiga tatarica assessment table shows 1986, 1988 entries; Yellow-eared Parrot first assessed 1994 (BirdLife); Siberian Taimen first global assessment 2012 (Wild Salmon Center).
