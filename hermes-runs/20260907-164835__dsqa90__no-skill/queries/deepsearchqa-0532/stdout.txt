## Five countries with the greatest share of reduction in reported TB cases in 2021 vs 2019

**India, Indonesia, China, Myanmar, and the Philippines** — these five countries accounted for **90% of the global reduction in TB case notifications in 2021 compared to 2019**, according to the WHO Global Tuberculosis Report (cited in CIDRAP and WHO sources).

## Which of these five reported the lowest number of TB cases in 2023?

**Myanmar** reported the lowest number of TB cases in 2023 among these five countries.

**2023 notified TB case numbers (new and relapse):**
- India: **2.55 million** (25.52 lakh) — *India TB Report 2024 / PIB*
- Indonesia: **~821,200** — *Statista/WHO data*
- China: **~700,000+** (estimated notifications; exact figure not directly published but higher than Myanmar)
- Philippines: **612,534** — *Philippines health authorities / WHO*
- Myanmar: **125,954** — *Myanmar TB Programmatic Achievement 2023 (The Global Fund Programme in Myanmar)*

Myanmar's 2023 notifications (~126,000) are substantially lower than the other four countries.
