Based on the USDA NASS 2012 and 2017 Census of Agriculture data (Table 37 for Maple Syrup), three maple-producing states saw a decrease in gallons produced from 2012 to 2017 while still producing at least 1,000 gallons in 2017:

| State | 2012 Production (gallons) | 2017 Production (gallons) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| **Ohio** | 94,133 | 93,672 | **−461** |
| **Illinois** | 3,331 | 2,384 | **−947** |
| **Virginia** | 1,800 | 1,385 | **−415** |

**Sources:**
- 2012 Census: Table 37 (Maple Syrup: 2012 and 2007) from `st99_2_037_037.pdf` — syrup produced column for 2012
- 2017 Census: State-level Table 40 (Woodland Crops Sales) and county-level Table 37 (Maple Syrup: 2017 and 2012) for each state

All other maple-producing states with ≥1,000 gallons in 2017 (Vermont, Maine, New York, Wisconsin, Pennsylvania, New Hampshire, Michigan, Massachusetts, Connecticut, Indiana, Kentucky, Maryland, West Virginia) showed increases from 2012 to 2017.
