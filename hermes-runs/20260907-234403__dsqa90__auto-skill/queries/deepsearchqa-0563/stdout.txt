Based on the official Texas Parks and Wildlife Department press release, there were **four artists from Austin** who participated in the 2023 "The Art of Texas State Parks" traveling art exhibit:

1. **Fidencio Duran** (Austin)
2. **Malou Flato** (Austin)
3. **Gordon Fowler** (Austin)
4. **Terri Wells** (Austin)

The full list of 30 participating artists included their hometowns, and these four were the only ones listed with Austin as their location.
