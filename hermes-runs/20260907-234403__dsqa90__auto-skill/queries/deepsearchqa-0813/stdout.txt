**Kuwait** had the highest average percentage of women and girls in its population from 2020 through 2023, at **39.05%**.

Of the 5 countries the World Bank recorded as having women and girls below 45% of total population in 2000 (UAE at 31.0%, Qatar at 31.7%, Bahrain at 40.9%, Kuwait at 41.5%, Oman at 41.7%), Kuwait's female share averaged highest over 2020–2023.
