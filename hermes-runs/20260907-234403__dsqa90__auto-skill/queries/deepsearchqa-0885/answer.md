Based on my research, the UK-born Nobel laureates in Economic Sciences (1969-2009, before Elinor Ostrom) who were affiliated with a UK institution at the time of their award, and have **fewer than 20 "Articles" on IDEAS/RePEc published prior to their award year** are:

| Laureate | Award Year | UK Institution at Award | Articles Before Award Year |
|----------|------------|------------------------|---------------------------|
| **James Meade** | 1977 | University of Cambridge | 11 |
| **Richard Stone** | 1984 | University of Cambridge | 12 |
| **Ronald Coase** | 1991 | London School of Economics | 13 |
| **James Mirrlees** | 1996 | University of Oxford / Cambridge | 8 |

**John Hicks (1972, Oxford)** has 27 articles before 1972, exceeding the threshold.

**Clive Granger (2003)** was affiliated with UC San Diego (US) at the time of his award, so does not meet the UK-institution criterion.
