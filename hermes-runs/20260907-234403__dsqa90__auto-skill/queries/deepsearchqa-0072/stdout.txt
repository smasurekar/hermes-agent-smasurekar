Based on the CDC's 2022 National Vital Statistics Report (Table 17), the following four US states had age-adjusted death rates exceeding all three thresholds in 2022:

| State | Heart Disease | Cancer | Diabetes |
|-------|--------------|--------|----------|
| **Arkansas** | 224.1 | 168.1 | 36.0 |
| **Mississippi** | 248.0 | 178.4 | 34.7 |
| **Oklahoma** | 257.1 | 171.6 | 33.8 |
| **West Virginia** | 209.5 | 176.3 | 41.1 |

All four states exceeded:
- Heart disease > 191.5 per 100,000
- Cancer > 161.9 per 100,000
- Diabetes > 33.1 per 100,000

Source: CDC/NCHS, *National Vital Statistics Reports*, Volume 74, Number 4, "Deaths: Final Data for 2022", Table 17 — age-adjusted death rates per 100,000 U.S. standard population.
