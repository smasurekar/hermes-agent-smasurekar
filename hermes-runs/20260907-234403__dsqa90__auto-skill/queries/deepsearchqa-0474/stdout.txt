Based on my research of the five 2007 Rock and Roll Hall of Fame performer inductees, **two artists are tied** for the longest-charting song on the Billboard Hot 100 prior to January 2024, each at **21 weeks**:

| Artist | Song | Weeks on Hot 100 | Peak Position |
|--------|------|------------------|---------------|
| **R.E.M.** | "Losing My Religion" | 21 weeks | #4 (1991) |
| **Van Halen** | "Jump" | 21 weeks | #1 (1984) |

The other inductees' longest runs:
- **Grandmaster Flash and the Furious Five** – "The Message" peaked at #62; Hot 100 weeks not explicitly stated in sources but well under 21 (UK data shows 9–11 weeks)
- **The Ronettes** – "Be My Baby": 13 weeks (peaked #2, 1963)
- **Patti Smith** – "Because the Night": 18 weeks (peaked #13, 1978)

Both "Losing My Religion" (R.E.M.) and "Jump" (Van Halen) spent exactly 21 weeks on the Billboard Hot 100.
