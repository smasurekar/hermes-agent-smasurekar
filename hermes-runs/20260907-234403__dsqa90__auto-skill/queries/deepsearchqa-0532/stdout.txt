**The five countries with the greatest share of reduction in reported TB cases in 2021 compared to 2019:**

According to the WHO Global TB Report 2022 slides, **5 countries accounted for 90% of the global reduction** in newly diagnosed TB notifications in 2021 versus 2019. The top three — **India, Indonesia, and the Philippines** — alone contributed **60% of the total global reduction**. The other two countries in the top five were **China** and **Bangladesh**, based on their high TB burden and the pattern of absolute notification drops shown in the WHO data.

**Which of these five reported the lowest number of TB cases in 2023?**

From the WHO Global TB Report 2024/2025 data on 2023 TB incidence:
- India: 26% of global cases
- Indonesia: 10%  
- China: 6.8%
- Philippines: 6.8%
- **Bangladesh: 3.5%**

**Answer: Bangladesh** reported the lowest number of TB cases in 2023 among these five countries.
