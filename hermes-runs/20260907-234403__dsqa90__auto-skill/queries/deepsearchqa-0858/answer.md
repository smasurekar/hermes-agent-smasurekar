Based on the TEA/AECOM 2023 Theme Index report data, **Epcot** saw the largest growth in attendance among Walt Disney World theme parks between 2022 and 2023.

**Walt Disney World park attendance changes (2022 → 2023):**

| Park | 2022 Attendance | 2023 Attendance | % Change |
|------|----------------|----------------|----------|
| **Epcot** | 10.0M | 11.98M | **+19.8%** |
| Magic Kingdom | 17.133M | 17.72M | +3.4% |
| Disney's Animal Kingdom | 9.027M | 8.77M | -2.8% |
| Disney's Hollywood Studios | 10.9M | 10.3M | -5.5% |

Epcot's 19.8% growth (nearly 2 million additional visitors) was driven largely by the opening of **Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind** in May 2022, which had its first full year of operation in 2023. The other three WDW parks all saw attendance declines year-over-year.
