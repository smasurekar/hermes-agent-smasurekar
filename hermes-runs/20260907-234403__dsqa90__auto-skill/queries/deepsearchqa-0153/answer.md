Based on IUCN Red List assessment history:

**Chronological order of earliest assessments:**

1. **Komodo Dragon** (*Varanus komodoensis*) — 1972 (first assessed as Vulnerable)
2. **Wild Water Buffalo** (*Bubalus arnee*) — 1986 (first assessed as Endangered)
3. **Yellow-eared Parrot** (*Ognorhynchus icterotis*) — 1994 (first assessed as Critically Endangered)
4. **Saiga tatarica** — 1996 (first assessed as Vulnerable)
5. **Siberian Taimen** (*Hucho taimen*) — 2012 (first global range-wide assessment)
