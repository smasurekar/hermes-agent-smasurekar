**Route 1 Spring Garden** is the bus route you're looking for.

It runs along Oxford Street and terminates at **Mumford Terminal** (Bay 2), which is right by the Walmart and Sobeys at Bayers Road Centre.

- **Oxford Street segment**: The route turns onto Oxford Street (right on Oxford from Coburg Road heading toward Mumford Terminal, or right on Oxford from Almon Street heading toward Bridge Terminal)
- **Terminal**: Mumford Terminal — the terminal building is directly adjacent to the Bayers Road Centre shopping area with Walmart and Sobeys
