**United States**

Based on WHO Global Health Estimates and CDC data (which feeds into WHO), the United States had coronary artery disease (ischemic heart disease) as the #1 cause of death for males in both 2020 and 2021. 

**Verification by country (top 5 economies per Madison Trust 2020):**
- **United States** – Heart disease remained the leading cause of death for males in both 2020 and 2021 (COVID-19 was 3rd for males)
- **China** – Stroke was the leading cause; ischemic heart disease ranked 2nd
- **Japan** – Stroke was the leading cause; ischemic heart disease ranked 2nd
- **Germany** – Coronary heart disease is the leading cause overall, but WHO sex-specific rankings for 2020–21 are less definitively published
- **United Kingdom** – COVID-19 was the leading cause of death overall in both 2020 and 2021, displacing ischemic heart disease for males in those years

The US is the only country among the top five where WHO/CDC data explicitly confirms ischemic heart disease held the #1 rank for males in both 2020 and 2021.
