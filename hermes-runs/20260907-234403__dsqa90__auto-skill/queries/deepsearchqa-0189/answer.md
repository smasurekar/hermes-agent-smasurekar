## Verification Notes

**Criterion 1 – Autism prevalence ≥ 2.21% (national average):**  
Dietz et al. (2020) reported a national average of 2.21% with state estimates ranging from 1.97% (Louisiana) to 2.42% (Massachusetts). The study explicitly identifies Massachusetts (2.42%), Virginia (2.41%), Connecticut (2.37%), California (2.36%), and Minnesota (2.35%) as the top five. World Population Review (citing the same study) additionally lists New Jersey, Illinois, Pennsylvania, Minnesota, California, Connecticut, and Virginia as states with prevalence "above 2.3%."

**Criterion 2 – 2023 unemployment ≥ 3.2%:**  
BLS annual averages for 2023 (released March 2024) show the national rate at 3.6%. The six states above all had rates at or above 3.2% (PA 3.4%, MA 3.4%, CT 3.8%, CA 4.8%, IL 4.5%, NJ 4.4%). Notably, Minnesota (2.8%) and Virginia (2.9%) — two other high-autism states — fall below the 3.2% threshold.

**Criterion 3 – All electors voted for the Democratic nominee in both 2016 and 2020:**  
In 2016, only Washington (4 Democratic electors voted for Powell/Spotted Eagle) and Hawaii (1 elector voted for Sanders) had faithless Democratic votes that were actually counted. Maine, Minnesota, and Colorado had attempted faithless votes that were invalidated/replaced. In 2020, there were zero faithless electors. All six states above voted Democratic in both elections and had zero faithless votes cast in either election.
