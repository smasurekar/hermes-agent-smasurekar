Based on the Statistics Canada data (Table 34-10-0063-01), here are the hydro-electric power plant capital expenditures by province for 2022 (in millions of dollars):

**2022:**
- British Columbia: **$2,742.0M**
- Quebec: $1,071.4M
- Ontario: $792.5M
- Manitoba: $302.2M
- Nova Scotia: $99.7M
- Newfoundland and Labrador: $42.2M
- Saskatchewan: $7.2M
- New Brunswick, Alberta, Yukon, NWT: suppressed (confidential)

**2023:**
- Ontario: $885.6M
- All other provinces/territories: suppressed (confidential)

Canada total: $5,190.3M (2022) and $5,017.0M (2023).

**Answer: British Columbia** had the largest hydro-electric capital expenditure in 2022 at **$2.74 billion** — over half of Canada's total. While most 2023 provincial figures are suppressed, BC's Site C project (a $16B project under active construction through 2023) and the StatCan article identifying BC as a major driver of hydro investment strongly indicate BC remained the largest in 2023 as well.
