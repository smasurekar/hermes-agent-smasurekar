The 7 artists in MoMA's 2009 Performance Exhibition Series were:
1. **Tehching Hsieh** (Taiwan)
2. **Simone Forti** (Italy/USA)
3. **Yvonne Rainer** (USA)
4. **Roman Ondák** (Slovakia)
5. **Mark Leckey** (UK/British)
6. **Fischerspooner** (USA)
7. **Joan Jonas** (USA)

Muslim population percentages by country (c. 2009):
- **UK: ~4.6–5%** (per Citizenship Survey 2008-09, Pew Research)
- Italy: ~4–5%
- USA: ~1–2%
- Taiwan: ~0.1%
- Slovakia: ~0.1%

**Mark Leckey** (Performance 5) comes from the United Kingdom, which had the highest percentage of Muslims among the artists' countries in 2009.
