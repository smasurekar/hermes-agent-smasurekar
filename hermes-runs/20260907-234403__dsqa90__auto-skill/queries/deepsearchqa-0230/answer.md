Based on my research of the Art Institute of Chicago's collection as of June 2024, **two** Georgia O'Keeffe works match all three criteria (New Mexico landscape, created before 1940, oil painting):

| Work | Year | Medium | Notes |
|------|------|--------|-------|
| **Black Cross, New Mexico** | 1929 | Oil on canvas | Depicts a handmade Catholic cross against rolling hills and sunset in New Mexico; purchased by the Art Institute in 1943 |
| **Red Hills with Flowers** | 1937 | Oil on canvas | Juxtaposes enlarged red-orange blossoms with the dry, steep hills surrounding her New Mexico home; bequeathed in 1992 |

**Works that didn't qualify:**
- *Cow's Skull with Calico Roses* (1931, oil on canvas) — still life with skull, not a landscape
- *White Shell with Red* (1938) — pastel on board, not oil
- *Road – Mesa with Mist* (1961), *The White Place in Sun* (1943), *Abiquiu Sand Hills and Mesa* (1945), *Sky above Clouds IV* (1965) — all created after 1940
- *Green Mountains, Canada* (1932), *Spring* (1923–24), *The Shelton with Sunspots, N.Y.* (1926), *Yellow Hickory Leaves with Daisy* (1928), *Blue and Green Music* (1919–21) — not New Mexico landscapes
