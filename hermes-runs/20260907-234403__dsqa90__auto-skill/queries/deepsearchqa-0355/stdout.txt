Based on the official Elections Canada results for the 44th Canadian General Election (2021), **two** British Columbia electoral districts had over 50% of valid ballots cast for the Conservative candidate:

| Electoral District | Conservative Candidate | % of Valid Ballots |
|-------------------|------------------------|-------------------|
| **Cariboo—Prince George** | Todd Doherty | **50.8%** |
| **Prince George—Peace River—Northern Rockies** | Bob Zimmer | **60.7%** |

All other BC ridings had Conservative vote shares below 50% (the next highest were Central Okanagan—Similkameen—Nicola at 47.6% and Abbotsford at 47.9%).
