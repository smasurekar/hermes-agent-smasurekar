Based on the Ripple of Hope Award laureates list from Wikipedia, the individuals who won the Ripple of Hope Award **before 2017** AND won a Nobel Prize **before 2010** are:

1. **Muhammad Yunus** — Ripple of Hope Award (2013), Nobel Peace Prize (2006)
2. **Al Gore** — Ripple of Hope Award (2011), Nobel Peace Prize (2007)
3. **Desmond Tutu** — Ripple of Hope Award (2008), Nobel Peace Prize (1984)

These three are the only laureates from the pre-2017 Ripple of Hope list who also received a Nobel Prize prior to 2010.
