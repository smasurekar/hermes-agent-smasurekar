The "earlier decision" Gorsuch criticized in his Denezpi dissent was **Gamble v. United States** (2019). In Gamble, Justice Ginsburg also dissented. In her Gamble dissent, she discussed the dual-sovereignty doctrine's foundation in **United States v. Lanza, 260 U.S. 377 (1922)**, which was then "twice-affirmed" — i.e., reaffirmed — in the 1959 companion cases **Bartkus v. Illinois** and **Abbate v. United States**. Ginsburg wrote: "I would not cling to those ill-advised decisions," making clear she believed **United States v. Lanza** (and its reaffirmations) should be overturned.
