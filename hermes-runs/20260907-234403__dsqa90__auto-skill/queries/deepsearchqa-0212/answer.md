Based on the research from authoritative sources (FishBase, NOAA Fisheries, Animal Diversity Web citing FAO Species Catalogue), the fish species documented to form mixed schools with Albacore tuna (*Thunnus alalunga*) with maximum lifespan ≤ 20 years are:

| Species | Scientific Name | Max Lifespan | Source |
|---------|-----------------|--------------|--------|
| Skipjack tuna | *Katsuwonus pelamis* | 12 years | FishBase / NOAA |
| Yellowfin tuna | *Thunnus albacares* | 18 years | FishBase / NOAA |
| Southern bluefin tuna | *Thunnus maccoyii* | 20 years | FishBase |
| Pacific bluefin tuna | *Thunnus orientalis* | 15 years | FishBase |

**Excluded (lifespan > 20 years):**
- Atlantic bluefin tuna (*Thunnus thynnus*) — documented to school with Albacore but max lifespan ~40 years

**Not verified as mixed-school associates:** Bigeye tuna, Pacific saury, anchovies — these appear in lifespan databases but lack explicit citations confirming mixed schooling with Albacore in the authoritative sources checked.
