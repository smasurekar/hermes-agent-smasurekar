Based on the official University of Cambridge Undergraduate Admissions Statistics for the 2019, 2021, and 2023 cycles (Home applicants from UK schools/colleges only), the following **9 subjects** saw an increase in the percentage of acceptances from Maintained schools in **both** the 2019→2021 and 2021→2023 periods:

| Subject | 2019 | 2021 | 2023 |
|---------|------|------|------|
| **Anglo-Saxon, Norse, & Celtic** | 53.3% | 58.3% | 62.5% |
| **Archaeology** | 68.8% | 80.0% | 84.6% |
| **Economics** | 59.8% | 66.7% | 69.6% |
| **History** | 60.8% | 71.5% | 73.1% |
| **History of Art** | 45.5% | 57.1% | 57.7% |
| **Land Economy** | 70.4% | 72.7% | 73.2% |
| **Theology, Religion & Philosophy of Religion** | 55.6% | 67.6% | 68.6% |
| **Computer Science** | 76.5% | 77.9% | 82.1% |
| **Natural Sciences** | 68.9% | 72.0% | 74.0% |

**Sources:**
- 2019 cycle: Table 4.3 "Proportion of Home applications, offers and acceptances to Cambridge from the maintained sector by subject 2019"
- 2021 cycle: Table 1.2 "Proportion of Home applications, offers and acceptances to Cambridge from the maintained sector by subject 2021"
- 2023 cycle: Table 2.2 "Proportion of Home applications, offers and acceptances to Cambridge from the maintained sector by subject 2023"

All three PDFs are published by the University of Cambridge at `undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/publications/undergraduate-admissions-statistics`.
