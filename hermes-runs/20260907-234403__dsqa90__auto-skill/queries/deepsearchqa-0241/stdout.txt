Based on my research, here are the U.S. Presidents who meet all three criteria:

**1. Theodore Roosevelt**
- Attended: **Harvard University** (AAU founding member, 1900)
- Governor: New York (1899–1900)
- Elected president: 1904 (succeeded McKinley in 1901, then won election in 1904)

**2. Woodrow Wilson**
- Attended: **Princeton University** (AAU founding member, 1900)
- Governor: New Jersey (1911–1913)
- Elected president: 1912

**3. Franklin D. Roosevelt**
- Attended: **Harvard University** (AAU founding member, 1900)
- Governor: New York (1929–1932)
- Elected president: 1932

**4. Jimmy Carter**
- Attended: **Georgia Institute of Technology** (AAU member since 2010)
- Governor: Georgia (1971–1975)
- Elected president: 1976

**5. George W. Bush**
- Attended: **Yale University** (AAU founding member, 1900)
- Governor: Texas (1995–2000)
- Elected president: 2000

---

**Excluded for failing one or more criteria:**
- **Calvin Coolidge** — Amherst College (not an AAU member)
- **Ronald Reagan** — Eureka College (not an AAU member)
- **Bill Clinton** — Georgetown University (not an AAU member)
- **Rutherford B. Hayes, Grover Cleveland, William McKinley** — Elected before 1900
