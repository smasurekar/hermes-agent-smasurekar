|-------|-----------|----------------|--------------|-----------------|-----------------|

| Tennessee | 18.4 | 47 | 33 | 23 | 23 |

| Oklahoma | 17.8 | 42 | 30 | 16 | 19 |

**Applying your criteria:**
1. **≥90% tested** → All 8 states qualify (100%)
2. **Composite ≥18.5** → Wyoming (19.0), Kentucky (18.7)
3. **English benchmark ≥42%** → Wyoming, Kentucky, Tennessee, Louisiana, Alabama, Oklahoma
4. **Math benchmark ≥19%** → All 8 states
5. **Reading benchmark ≥30%** → **None** — highest is Wyoming at 25%

**Answer: No states meet all five criteria.** The reading benchmark requirement (≥30%) eliminates every state — even the top performer (Wyoming) only had 25% of test-takers meet the reading benchmark.
