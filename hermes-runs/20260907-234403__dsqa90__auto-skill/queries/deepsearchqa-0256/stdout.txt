## Key Takeaways

- **Incineroar dominates raw stats** — 125 BST higher, crushing advantages in HP, Attack, Defense, and Sp. Atk.
- **Pachirisu's only edge** is Speed (95 vs 60), letting it function as a fast Follow Me redirector.
- **Sp. Def is identical (90)** — both are specially bulky for their roles.
- **Different eras, different roles**: Pachirisu (Gen 4) won as a niche support/redirector on a hyper-offense team. Incineroar (Gen 7) won as the premier Intimidate pivot with Fake Out, Parting Shot, and offensive presence — a role it has held in VGC for 7+ years.

Wolfey hates Incineroar because it warps the metagame around itself; Pachirisu was a one-off Cinderella story.
