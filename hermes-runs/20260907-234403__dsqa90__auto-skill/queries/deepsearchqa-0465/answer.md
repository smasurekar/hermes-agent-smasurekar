Based on the NAWE website, **Writing in Education Vol. 33 "Writing & Location"** contains the following articles and authors:

**Article authors (contributors):**
- **Bryan Podmore** — 2 articles: "A Risky Business This Writing?" and "Crashing through the Fourth Wall"
- **Sarah Wardle** — 2 articles: "Audere Est Facere" and "Reflections on Memory and Identity"
- **Martin Goodman** — "Location and Dislocation - a Writer's Use of Place"
- **Fred Sedgwick** — "Primary Children Writing in the Grip of Shakespeare"
- **Paul Munden** — "Text and Context"
- **Maggie Butt** — "The View from Here"
- **Keith Bennett** — "Welcome to Maribor"
- **Carl Tighe** — "Worlds in War: War in Words" (Inaugural Lecture as Professor of Creative Writing at University of Derby)

**Also featured as respondents** in Podmore's "A Risky Business This Writing?": Susan Wicks, Michelene Wandor, Gillian Allnutt, and Graham Mort.

(Note: There's also a **Vol. 31 "Writing & Location"** with a different set of authors — Fred Sedgwick, Margaret Anne Clarke, Rupert Loydell, Ade Solanke, Jenny Webb, Hazel Moffat, Mike Ferguson, Fiona Coe, David Orme, Marianne Wheelaghan — but you asked specifically about Vol. 33.)
