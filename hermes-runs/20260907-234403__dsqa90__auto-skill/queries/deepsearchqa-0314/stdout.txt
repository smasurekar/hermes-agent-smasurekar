| Species | Author, Year | Status |
|---------|--------------|--------|
| ***Tyrannosaurus mcraeensis*** | Dalman *et al.*, 2024 | Described Jan 2024 (Hall Lake Formation, New Mexico). Validity **disputed** — Morrison *et al.* (2025) and Voris *et al.* (2025) argue it falls within *T. rex* variation; Carr (2025) agrees. Taxonomy box lists it with a "?" |

### **Proposed in 2022, not accepted by consensus (2 species)**
| Species | Author, Year | Status |
|---------|--------------|--------|
| ***Tyrannosaurus imperator*** | Paul *et al.*, 2022 | Based on "robust" morph (holotype = "Sue"). **Rejected** by Brusatte, Carr, Holtz, Hone, O'Connor, Zanno (2022) as intraspecific variation. Paul reiterated in 2025; still not accepted. |
| ***Tyrannosaurus regina*** | Paul *et al.*, 2022 | Based on "gracile" morph (holotype = "Wankel rex"). Same criticism as *T. imperator*; not accepted. |

### **Bottom line for May 2024**
- **1 undisputed species**: *T. rex*
- **1 newly named but contested**: *T. mcraeensis* (with "?" in formal taxonomy)
- **2 proposed but rejected by the community**: *T. imperator*, *T. regina*

No other species of *Tyrannosaurus* were considered valid by the paleontological mainstream as of May 2024.
